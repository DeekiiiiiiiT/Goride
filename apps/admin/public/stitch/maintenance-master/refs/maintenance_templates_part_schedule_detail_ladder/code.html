Inspection vs Replacement

Routine garage visits measure fluid conductometry and optical moisture levels. Even when readings pass tolerance, the hard system expiration is reached at 24 months.

SPECIFICATION: Glycol-ether DOT 4 fluid with minimum dry equilibrium reflux boiling point of 230°C (446°F).
