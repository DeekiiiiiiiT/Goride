Timing belt NOT ON THIS CAR

Chain-driven engine. 1KR-FE utilizes silent internal timing chain lubricated by engine oil; no scheduled replacement interval required under normal life expectancy.

remove_circle_outline
Rear disc brake pads NOT ON THIS CAR

Equipped with leading-trailing rear drum brake shoes (Toyota OEM Ref: 04495-B2400). Serviced via friction lining thickness inspection rather than pad replacement.

remove_circle_outline
Differential fluid (Rear) NOT ON THIS CAR

2WD Front-wheel drive transaxle configuration (M900A). Integrated front differential shares CVT oil sump; separate rear hypoid differential unit absent.

remove_circle_outline
Fuel filter (in-line) NOT ON THIS CAR

Non-serviceable in-tank fuel pump strainer module. Does not feature external chassis line filter canister; serviced exclusively upon fuel pump unit malfunction.
