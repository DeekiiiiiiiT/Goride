ROAM DOMINION — MAINTENANCE: MAY NEED + SUPPLIES FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Dominion - Maintenance templates · May need + Supplies (Desktop Admin, for the owner and catalog staff):

Design modern, premium screens that let the owner say which parts and shop supplies each maintenance job may need. They sit inside the "Maintenance templates" section of the Dominion admin console, next to the Jobs tab. A JOB is one piece of maintenance work, e.g. "Throttle Body Cleaning" (a Service) or "Intake Manifold Gasket" (a Part that goes onto the car). A SUPPLY is something used up while doing the work and the same for every car, e.g. "Throttle body cleaner" (sold by the can), "Brake cleaner", "Shop rags". Each job gets a "May need" list of parts and supplies, each marked "Usually" or "Sometimes", with an optional usual quantity ("1 can") and a short note. When someone logs that job in the Roam Fleet app, the list appears so they can tick what was actually used. "May need" is different from the existing "Also do" list: Also do suggests separate work; May need is what gets used up inside this one job, and a part can't be in both. Nothing is deleted: supplies are retired and can be restored. Dominion only holds this list; it never shows prices, suppliers, stock, fleet names, plates, usage numbers from the apps, percentages, charts or side panels. The console should feel like a polished, modern SaaS product in the style of Linear, Stripe Dashboard and Notion: airy, calm and pleasant to use every day. Each screen is one single view. Make every screen tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling and nothing cut off.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Modern and calm: generous white space (24px inside cards, 32px between sections), clear hierarchy, nothing cramped
- Plain words first; codes small and soft grey
- Every action is a real button with a fill or a visible outline — never a text link. Remove, Retire and Cancel are red (red outline, red text; solid red only for a final confirm). One blue primary button per screen
- No browser pop-ups; every confirm is inside the app
- Everything works by keyboard: list order has move-up / move-down arrow buttons as well as drag; pop-ups move focus in and back out
- Use only the content listed under each screen — no extra panels, charts, statistics or made-up numbers

- Soft red inline messages for errors; toast after save: "Saved. Throttle Body Cleaning now lists 3 items."

INTERACTIONS:
- "+ Add item" opens Screen 3; "Add" closes it and the new row appears at the bottom of the May need list
- "Edit" on a May need row reopens the pop-up with the item fixed; only need, quantity and note can change
- "Remove" takes the row off straight away and shows a toast "Removed from May need. Old logs keep it."
- Arrow buttons and drag change the order; this is the order Roam Fleet shows
- "+ New supply" opens the supply drawer and returns with the new supply selected
- "Retire" opens Screen 7; "Restore" brings a supply back with its old May-need rows
- In the "Also do" picker, a part already in May need is faded with "Already in May need"
- Esc closes pop-ups and drawers; focus returns to the button that opened them
