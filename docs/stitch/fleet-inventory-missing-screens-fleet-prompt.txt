ROAM FLEET — INVENTORY & ASSET MANAGEMENT: MISSING SCREENS (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Inventory & Asset Management, missing screens (Desktop Web App, for fleet owners, fleet managers and their staff):

Design the 15 remaining high-fidelity screens for the Inventory & Asset Management section of the Roam Fleet app. The main tabs (Overview, Stock, Vehicle kits, Assets, Movements, Purchasing, Counts, Alerts) and the Issue, Receive, Transfer/Return/Write-off dialogs are already designed; these new screens must sit beside them and look like the same product. Each screen opens from a button that already exists on those tabs (named under every screen below). Stock never changes by typing over a number: it changes only through Receive, Issue, Return, Transfer, Count or Write-off, and every change is kept in a history that cannot be edited. Large changes (adjustments, write-offs, counts with big differences, purchase orders over the approval limit) wait for a second person to approve; the person who asked can never approve their own request. The tone is calm, plain and exact: a well-run storeroom logbook, not an accounting system.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first (1440px wide), with a phone version
- Keep what works: the existing tab bar, the black primary button in the top-right, and the Stock table layout stay recognisable; the "Bulk Assignment" button becomes "Issue" in the same spot
- Stock is never typed over: quantities are read-only everywhere; every change goes through Receive, Issue, Return, Transfer, Count or Write-off
- Every number can be clicked to see the history behind it
- Preview before anything changes: every action dialog shows a "What will change" box before the confirm button
- Kits are the heart of the page: for each car, what it should have, what it has, what's missing
- Plain words first: "Missing 3 items — Tyre pump, First-aid kit, Warning triangle", never "compliance 0.7"
- No internal ids, UUIDs or table names anywhere
- One verb per action: "Issue" on the button, the dialog title and the confirm button
- One save: confirm buttons show a spinner and cannot be pressed twice
- Corrections never delete: a wrong movement is undone with a "Reverse" that shows in the history
- Light theme default, dark mode variant
- 8px spacing grid, 12px card corners, hairline borders, soft shadows, skeleton loading
- New screens are dialogs (centred, 640–880px wide) or right-side drawers (560px) over the existing Inventory page, unless a screen says "full page"
- Every dialog: title, one-line plain explanation, the form, a "What will change" box, then Cancel (outline) + one black confirm button using the same verb as the title
- Required fields are marked; errors show under the field in red, in plain words
- Nothing is deleted: items, vendors, locations and bins are "Archived" and can be restored
