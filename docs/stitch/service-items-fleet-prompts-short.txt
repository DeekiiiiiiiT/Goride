ROAM FLEET — LOG SERVICE: ITEMS USED FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Log service · Items used (Desktop Web App, for fleet owners and their staff):

Design modern, premium screens for recording the parts and supplies a job used up, inside the "Log service" dialog of Fleet maintenance in the Roam Fleet app, plus the service history that goes with it. The person using it is a normal car-fleet owner, not a mechanic. When they tick a job such as "Throttle Body Cleaning", a short "Items used" list appears that Roam has prepared for that job (for example "Throttle body cleaner", usually 1 can, and "Throttle Body Gasket", sometimes). Nothing is ticked for them: they tick what was actually used, set how many and what each cost (or leave the cost blank if they don't know), and it is added to the bill. They can add something that isn't on the list with "+ Other item". The dialog keeps its four steps: Date & mileage, Work done, Details, Review. Service history shows the items used, and a service that didn't finish saving shows a "Finish saving" button. The app should feel like a polished consumer product in the style of Linear, Stripe and Airbnb: friendly, airy and easy to scan. Each screen is one single view. Make every screen tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling and nothing cut off.

BRAND IDENTITY:
- Roam Fleet web app: a clean top bar with "Roam Fleet"; the page "Fleet maintenance" with a friendly orange wrench icon; the Log service dialog floats over a softly blurred, dimmed page
- LIGHT MODE (default):
  - Primary: Near-black (#030213) — primary buttons, the current step, selected choices
  - Background: very light grey (#f8fafc); cards and dialogs white
  - Text: #0a0a0a; helper text soft grey #71717a
  - Success: Emerald (#059669)
  - Attention / not finished: Amber (#d97706)
  - Danger (Cancel, Remove): Red (#d4183d)
  - Soft tints for pills and icon tiles: Slate 100 (#f1f5f9)
- DARK MODE:
  - Background: #18181b; cards: #232327; borders: #3f3f46
  - Text: #fafafa; helper text: #a1a1aa
  - Primary: #fafafa (buttons become light with dark text)
  - Success: #34d399; attention: #f59e0b; danger: #f87171
- Shapes: cards and dialogs 16px rounded corners; buttons and inputs 10px; chips are fully rounded pills
- Depth: soft, light shadows on cards and dialogs; at most a very light 1px border
- Typography: Inter. Titles 20–24px semibold, body 15px, helper text 13px. Sentence case everywhere — no all-caps labels, no monospace text. Money uses lined-up (tabular) numbers.
- Money is Jamaican dollars: "J$ 1,200"; kilometres "197,486 km"; dates "12 Nov 2026"
- Icons: friendly line icons (Lucide style) on soft-tinted round tiles
- Pills: "Supply", "Part", "Clean", "Declined", "Voided" soft grey; "Usually used" soft slate fill; "Fits this car" and "Verified" soft green; "Not finished saving" and "Check before buying" soft amber

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Modern and friendly: generous white space (24px inside cards, 32px between sections), clear hierarchy, nothing cramped
- Plain words for a normal person: "Items used", "Cost each", "Leave a cost blank if you don't know it" — no codes, jargon or technical labels
- Never tick anything for the user; suggestions stay suggestions
- The money adds up in front of them: each item shows its amount, each job shows "Job J$ X + items J$ Y = J$ Z"
- Every action is a real button with a fill or a visible outline — never a text link. Cancel and Remove are red (red outline, red text). One solid black primary button per screen
- No browser pop-ups; every message is inside the app
- Desktop-first (1440px), with a phone version

ADDITIONAL UI COMPONENTS:
- Item row: checkbox, icon tile, name, kind pill, quantity stepper with unit, "Cost each" money input, optional "Brand", amount
- Quantity stepper: round "−" and "+" buttons around the number; the unit follows the number ("1 can", "2 cans")
- Picked-part card with "Fits this car" check and outline "Change" button
- Summary pill "Job J$ X + items J$ Y = J$ Z"
- Amber "Not finished saving" pill with clock icon
- In-app message boxes and toasts (no browser pop-ups)

INTERACTIONS:
- Ticking an item shows its quantity, cost and brand fields, with the usual quantity filled in; unticking hides them and takes the amount out of the total
- "Change" / "Pick part" opens Screen 2; "Use this part" fills the row; "Type it in instead" shows "Brand" and "Part number (optional)" inputs
- "+ Other item" adds an editable row; "Remove" takes it away
- Marking a job "Declined" hides its Items used and clears its ticks
- "Log service" saves once: the button shows a spinner and can't be pressed twice
- "Finish saving" finishes a service that didn't finish; it never makes a second service
- "Reload" loads the latest version of the service; "Cancel" closes without saving
- Esc closes pop-ups; everything works with the keyboard
