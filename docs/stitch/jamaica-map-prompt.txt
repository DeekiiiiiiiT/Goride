OVERALL LOOK — high fidelity, modern

A modern, calm GIS console in the Dominion design system. Think Mapbox Studio or Felt, made quiet and precise.

- The map is the hero: large, edge-to-edge inside its card, with floating controls on white surfaces (soft shadow, 12px radius, hairline border).
- Page background is a very light cool grey. Cards are white with a hairline border and a very soft shadow, 12–16px radius.
- Strict 8px spacing grid.
- Type: Dominion sans for text. Dominion mono with tabular figures for every code (JM06), count, area and date.

Colours are restrained:
- Neutral greys almost everywhere.
- Dominion blue for selection, links and primary buttons.
- Parish borders: a dark slate line (2px).
- Town borders: a mid-grey line (1.25px).
- Communities: a light grey dashed line (1px).
- The selected area: a 2.5px Dominion-blue outline with a 10% blue fill. Its parent gets a faint grey fill.
- Green only for a "Published" chip. Amber only for drafts or warnings.

Micro-details: hairline dividers, small pill chips, focus rings, skeleton shimmer, subtle hover lift.

Keep it real:
- No glassmorphism, no gradients, no 3D terrain, no illustrations, no AI badges.
- No "REGION" badge, no "PARTITION" label, no "LOC:" footer.
- No delivery or market words.

Shell: the existing Dominion admin shell. The left sidebar is dark navy with "Places and Maps" selected. The top bar is white, with the search field "Search entities, engines, routes…" and the avatar "DT · Deeki T · Owner".
