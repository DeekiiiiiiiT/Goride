ROAM FLEET — TOLLS STEP, ONE PART AT A TIME — FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Tolls step in Week Reconciliation (Desktop Web App with a phone layout, for the fleet owner and fleet manager):

Redesign ONE area of step 3 "Tolls" inside "Week Reconciliation" in Roam Fleet, the same way the Fuel step was redesigned. Today the Tolls step shows every group stacked in one long page (Suggested matches, Pick the right trip, Tolls with no trip, cash receipts, duplicates, paybacks with no toll, Decided, then the Seal box). The person scrolls and scrolls and loses their place. The new design shows ONE part at a time as a large card. A short progress row at the top shows the six parts and where the person is. When they finish a part they click "Next" and the card slides to the next part; "Back" slides to the previous one. The last card, "Seal tolls", ends with the "Seal tolls" button. The rule behind every part: on a trip, the passenger is always the one who owes the toll, never the fleet. Uber pays it back on the trip; if Uber pays short, the driver disputes the rest with Uber; on an InDrive cash trip the driver collects it from the rider. The fleet or the driver only pays when that fails or when there was no trip. Everything outside this area stays exactly as it is today: the app frame, the left step rail, the week line, the title "Tolls" with "Who pays each toll this week?", the five money tiles, the "3 of 15 tolls decided" bar, and the right-hand column. Only the area between the progress bar and the footer changes. The person using this is not an accountant: each card says in plain words what it is for, what needs doing, and the one or two buttons that do it. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best business banking apps. Every screen is tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- One part at a time. Only one card is on screen; the others are reached with Next / Back or by clicking a part in the progress row.
- The person always knows where they are and what is left: the progress row shows all six parts with their status ("Done", "3 to confirm", "12 to send", "Nothing to do").
- Moving is never blocked: Next always works, so the person can look ahead. Only "Seal tolls" waits until every part is clear, and the last card says exactly what is still open with a button that jumps to that part.
- Every card answers three things at the top: what this part is for (one sentence), its status chip, and what to do now.
- A part with nothing in it still shows, with a calm green "Nothing to do" state, so the person sees it was checked.
- Disputes never block sealing: a toll that is being disputed with Uber counts as decided for now, and the week can still close on time.
- The money always adds up, and the money tiles and right column stay visible the whole time.
- Calm, not alarming: red only for things that block sealing or a dispute about to run out of days.
- Slides are short and smooth (250 ms, ease-out, the new card slides in from the right on Next and from the left on Back).
- One view per screen.

ADDITIONAL UI COMPONENTS:
- Progress row: six fully rounded pills on one line, joined by a 2px line; each pill shows number, short name and a status (green check "Done", blue dot "N to confirm", amber "N to send" / "N to decide" / "N to check", slate "Nothing to do", grey padlock "Not ready"). The current pill is near-black with white text. Every pill is a button that slides to that part.

- Part card: white, 20px radius, soft shadow, 28px padding; header = title + subtitle on the left, status chip on the right; content; footer row with Back, "Part N of 6", Next.
- Next button text always names the next part: "Next: Disputes →".
- Toll row: tag or banknote icon, date and time, place, plate chip, driver, amount right-aligned, payment chip ("Tag" / "Cash"), trip pill, status chip, buttons on the right.
- Dispute row: as a toll row plus the money line "Toll · Uber paid · to dispute", a state chip ("Not sent yet" amber, "Sent <date>" blue, "No refund after 2 pay periods" red, "Won" green, "Lost · driver pays" slate) and days left ("8 of 9 days left", red at 2 days or fewer).

INTERACTIONS:
- "Next" slides the current card out to the left and the next card in from the right (250 ms); "Back" does the reverse. The progress row and bar update at the same moment.
- Clicking a pill in the progress row slides straight to that part.
- The step opens on the first part that still needs something; if everything is done it opens on "Seal".
- Deciding something inside a card (Confirm, Confirm all, Driver pays, Fleet pays, Mark as sent, Check receipt) updates that card's chip, the matching pill, the money tiles and the right column at once, without leaving the card.
- "Confirm all", "Confirm all Driver pays", "Lost" and "Undo" open an in-app dialog that lists what will happen and the total, with one primary confirm button.
- "Refund received" opens an in-app dialog listing Uber's refund lines for that driver near that date; the person picks one.
- On the Seal card, "Go to …" buttons slide to that part.
- "Seal tolls" opens an in-app dialog: "Seal tolls for the week of 9 Nov 2026? Spend J$5,410.00 · Paid back J$5,170.00 · Waiting on Uber J$240.00." with a primary "Seal tolls"; then Screen 9.
- The keyboard works too: left and right arrow keys move Back / Next when no text box is focused.
- On the phone the Back / Next bar stays at the bottom of the screen.
