ROAM DOMINION — VEHICLE PICTURES — FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Dominion - Vehicle Pictures (Desktop Admin, for the owner and catalogue editors, who are not mechanics):

Design a new "Vehicle pictures" page inside the Vehicles section of the Roam Dominion admin console.

What Dominion is:
- Roam Dominion is Roam's master database. It builds the reference data that every Roam app reads: cars, makers, parts, specs, and now car pictures.
- Dominion never shows anything from the apps: no fleets, no customers, no vehicles, plates, drivers or counts of use. This page shows only the pictures themselves.

What the page is for:
- Roam keeps one studio picture for each car and colour, for example "White Toyota Roomy, 1st generation". The apps read these pictures from Dominion.
- This page is where Roam staff look after the pictures:
  - see them all in one gallery
  - spot one that came out wrong and make a new one
  - upload a better one
  - put back the previous one
- The owner can also switch the AI picture maker on or off, see this month's picture spend, and remake every picture after a prompt change.

Look and feel:
- Calm, modern and visual, like a photo library in a premium app. The pictures are the hero.
- Each screen is tall enough to show everything with no inner scrolling.
- Draw each screen in light mode. Dark mode uses the colours below.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first at 1440px; content column max 1200px. One phone screen (390px) is included.
- The gallery is the page. Counts and filters sit above it in one calm row; nothing pushes the pictures below the fold.
- Each picture card says in plain words which car and colour it is, and how and when it was made. Nothing else.
- Clicking a card opens a right-side drawer (560px) with the large picture and every action for it.
- Owner-only controls (the AI on/off switch, remake all) sit in one "AI settings" card at the top right, clearly separate from everyday picture fixes.
- Every action that costs money says the cost before it runs ("About US$0.04").
- Every change can be undone for 30 days ("Restore previous"), and the dialogs say so.
- A picture the apps already use can't be removed; its Remove button is disabled with a short reason.

INTERACTIONS:
- Clicking a count tile filters the gallery; clicking it again clears the filter.
- "Open" on a card slides in the drawer (Screen 2); Escape or the close icon closes it.
- "Looks good" / "Looks wrong" set the rating at once (the chip updates); "Looks wrong" also highlights the "Regenerate" button.
- "Regenerate" opens Screen 3; after confirming, the card shows "Being made" and swaps to the new picture by itself when ready.
- "Upload replacement" opens Screen 4 inside the drawer; "Use this picture" swaps it in and adds a version row "Uploaded by staff".
- "Restore previous" asks in a small in-app dialog ("Put back version 1? You can undo this for 30 days." with "Cancel" red outline and "Restore" primary), then adds a version row "Restored".
- "Remove picture" is enabled only when the apps don't use the picture; it opens Screen 5.
- Turning the AI switch off confirms in an in-app dialog ("Turn off AI pictures? Roam stops making new pictures; existing pictures stay." with "Cancel" secondary and "Turn off" solid red).
- "Remake all from older prompt" opens Screen 6 before anything is queued.
