==================== FIRST PASTE ====================

Roam Fleet - Find parts, part pictures (desktop web app with a phone layout, for car owners, fleet owners and their staff):

Design high-fidelity, modern screens that add a real-looking picture of the part to the answers in Roam Fleet's "Find parts" tab. The person using it is not a mechanic. They pick their car, say what they need (for example "front brake pads") and get one plain answer: the part, a Verified badge, and one number to give the store. Today the answer shows a simple flat drawing of a brake pad for every part. Now, when Roam has one, the answer shows a realistic studio photo of that exact part, so the person can see what they are looking for before they go to the store. The photo is made by AI from the car maker's drawing, so a short caption always says so. When Roam has no photo yet, the simple drawing stays. It must feel friendly, bright and trustworthy, like the best modern shopping and banking apps. Make each screen tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling. Draw only the screens asked for in this message. Information only: no prices, shops, stock, quotes or carts anywhere.

ADDITIONAL UI COMPONENTS:
- PartPictureTile (shared, one component for all four answer layouts): props `picture: { url, urlDetail } | null`, `fallback` (the existing Stitch illustration for that part type, e.g. StitchPadIllustration). With a picture: `<button>` wrapping the card-size image (alt = part name), caption under it; tap opens the large view. Without: the fallback drawing, no caption, not clickable.
- PartPictureOverlay: in-app overlay with the detail-size image, name, number in mono, caption, primary "Close"; Escape closes; focus returns to the tile.
- Image loading: show the soft tile with the fallback drawing until the photo has loaded; if the photo fails to load, keep the drawing (never a broken-image icon).

INTERACTIONS:
- Tapping the photo opens Screen 2's large view; "Close" or Escape shuts it.
- The photo comes from `fleet_parts_lookup` (plan §3.6): one `picture` per answer card for that part's group, only when it is ready and not hidden in Dominion. When Dominion hides it, the next load shows the drawing with no caption.
- "Other numbers stores may use" rows never show a picture.
