ROAM FLEET — MAINTENANCE: 2 SCREENS TO REDRAW (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Maintenance, redraw (Desktop Web App, for fleet owners, fleet managers and their staff):

Redraw two screens of Roam Fleet's Maintenance area. The other screens are already designed (Due items drawer for one car with source chips, "Why this schedule?" popover, Set custom schedule dialog, Maintenance alerts bell, Overview rows, States, Phone) — these two must look like the same product. Service schedules come from Roam's master database; each due line says where its schedule came from ("Car maker", "Part maker" or "Roam policy"). Roam Fleet has NO telematics: people type in the odometer. A car with no odometer reading yet shows date-only due dates and "Waiting for odometer" instead of an invented distance. A car can be marked "Hard use" (taxi, delivery, heavy traffic) to use shorter "severe duty" schedules where Roam has them. Draw each screen so the WHOLE screen is visible: nothing cut off at the top or bottom, nothing covered by a toast or another layer.
