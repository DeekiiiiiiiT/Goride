ROAM FLEET — WEEK RECONCILIATION — FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Week Reconciliation (Desktop Web App with a phone layout, for the fleet owner, fleet manager and fleet accountant):

Design a complete, modern, high-fidelity redesign of the "Week Reconciliation" section inside Roam Fleet. Once a week, the fleet manager takes one Monday-to-Sunday week, for one service line (Rideshare, or Delivery), from "files uploaded" to "every driver paid or collected, and the week's books locked". It happens on ONE screen, in EIGHT steps, in this order: 1 Upload inputs, 2 Assign service lines, 3 Tolls, 4 Tag account, 5 Fuel, 6 Trips & earnings, 7 Settlement preview, 8 Close week. The person using it is not an accountant. Every step answers one plain question ("Who pays each toll?", "Who owes whom this week?"), shows one clear next action, and never sends them to another page to finish. Today the section feels scattered: steps open whole other apps inside them, there are several different ways to close a week, and screens disagree about numbers. The new design must feel like one calm, guided desk: a left step rail, one step in the middle, and a small summary on the right. Numbers are big and clear, every problem says what it is, how much money it is, and which button fixes it. Nothing is ever hidden, and nothing is ever vague.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- One screen for the whole week. No step ever opens another app, another wizard or a second set of steps inside it. There is exactly ONE step rail.
- One question per step, written as the step's subtitle. One obvious primary button per step.
- Every problem is a row: what it is in plain words, the amount in J$, and one button that fixes it ("Go to Tolls", "Upload", "Decide").
- Numbers never disagree: the week card, the step rail, the step and the Close box always show the same count.
- The page always says how fresh the numbers are ("Numbers updated 2 min ago") and loudly says when they are not.
- Nothing is ever hidden: weeks never drop off the list because they are old; locked steps can still be opened read-only to see why.
- Approvals are honest: you can send a decision to another approver, or approve it yourself with a written reason, which is clearly labelled "Self-approved".
- Closed weeks are locked. The only way to change a closed week is a correction (restatement) that someone signs, and every change is listed in History.
- Calm, not alarming: red is used only for things that truly block closing.

INTERACTIONS:
- **Open week / week card click:** opens the desk on the first step that needs work.
- **Step rail row:** opens that step; locked steps open read-only with their reason.
- **Continue to <next step>:** completes the current step and moves to the next.
- **Upload:** opens the upload panel in place, never another page; after upload the source chip turns "Received" once the numbers update.
- **Mark no activity:** asks once in a small in-app dialog which empty days to mark, then the chip turns "No activity".
- **Remind Roam:** sends the reminder and the card shows "Reminded 10 Nov 2026".
- **Toll row buttons:** decide the toll; the row moves to its new section with a short slide.
- **Seal tolls / Finalize fuel:** completes the step; the rail shows "Complete".
- **Pay / Collect / Settle from balance / Write off:** open small in-app confirm dialogs; Write off asks for a reason.
- **Close week, Waive, Accept statement cash, Write off, Reopen, Sign restatement:** open the approval dialog (Screen 12).
- **Sign correction:** one button, no reason needed; the correction moves into History as "Signed by <name>".
- **Reload week:** reloads the step and keeps what the user typed.
- **All confirmations are in-app dialogs, never browser pop-ups.**
- **Keyboard:** every row and button reachable with Tab, Enter opens, Esc closes dialogs, visible focus rings.
