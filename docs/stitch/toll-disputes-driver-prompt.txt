ROAM DRIVER — DISPUTE A TOLL WITH UBER — FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Driver - Tolls to dispute with Uber (Phone App, 390px, for drivers of fleet cars):

Design high-fidelity phone screens for a new "Tolls to dispute" part of the Roam Driver app. When a driver goes through a toll during an Uber trip, the passenger owes the toll and Uber pays it back to the driver. Sometimes Uber pays back less than the toll cost (for example the Portmore toll costs J$390.00 and Uber paid back J$370.00, so J$20.00 is still owed). Uber only lets the driver ask for the rest from inside the Uber driver app, within 9 days of the trip. Roam spots each short toll by itself and shows the driver a card with everything needed: when and where the toll was, how much is still owed, and a ready-made message to paste into Uber. The driver taps "Copy text", taps "Open Uber App", finds the trip in Uber and pastes the message, then comes back and taps "Mark as sent". That is all: the fleet sees the result when Uber's refund arrives. Each card also says plainly what happens if the driver does nothing. The tone is friendly, short and reassuring, like a helpful reminder, never a telling-off. A driver should finish one dispute in under a minute while parked. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best consumer banking apps. Every screen is tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling.


BRAND IDENTITY:
- Match the existing Roam Driver app (do not redesign it):
  - white cards on a light slate page, rounded corners, large touch targets (at least 48px tall)
  - the app's top bar (menu button on the left, "Roam" in the middle, bell and profile on the right) and its bottom tab bar stay as they are
  - this feature lives in two places: a card on the Home screen, and its own page "Tolls to dispute" opened from that card or from the menu under "My Expenses"
- LIGHT MODE (default):
  - Primary: Emerald 600 (#059669) — primary buttons, ticks, the active tab
  - Background: Slate 100 (#f1f5f9)
  - Surface cards: White (#ffffff) with a soft shadow (0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.04), 0 8px 24px rgba(15,23,42,0.06)) and a hairline border #e2e8f0
  - Text primary: Slate 900 (#0f172a)
  - Muted text: Slate 600 (#475569)
  - Money still owed / to dispute: Amber 700 (#b45309) on a soft amber #fffbeb
  - Sent, waiting on Uber: Blue 700 (#1d4ed8) on #eff6ff
  - Last days / time is up: Red 600 (#dc2626) on #fef2f2
  - Done: Emerald 600 (#059669) on #ecfdf5
- DARK MODE:
  - Background: #0f172a
  - Surface: #1e293b
  - Border: #334155
  - Text primary: #f8fafc
  - Muted text: #cbd5e1
  - Primary: #10b981
  - Amber #fbbf24 on #451a03; blue #60a5fa on #172554; red #f87171 on #450a0a
- Shapes: cards 16px corner radius, buttons 12px, chips fully rounded.
- Typography: Inter. Card title 17px semibold, body 15px, helper text 13px. Money uses tabular figures, always "J$" with 2 decimals (J$20.00). The plate is shown in a small grey rounded box.
- Dates and times in plain words: "9 Nov, 7:15 AM", "by Mon 18 Nov". Never "2026-11-09", never codes or ids.
- Status chips (rounded, small, always words):
  - "Not sent yet" — amber
  - "Sent · waiting on Uber" — blue
  - "1 day left" — red
  - "Time is up" — red
- Buttons always look like buttons:
  - Primary = solid emerald with white text ("Mark as sent").
  - Secondary = white with a visible #cbd5e1 border and dark text ("Copy text", "Open Uber App").
  - Never underlined text links, never grey text-only buttons. Every confirm is an in-app bottom sheet, never a browser pop-up.
- No gradients, glassmorphism or 3D. No AI badges.

  - The message to copy (shown in full in a soft grey box): "Hi, on 9 Nov 2026 at 7:15 AM I drove through the Portmore toll during this trip. The toll cost J$390.00 and I was refunded J$370.00, so J$20.00 is still owed. Please refund the remaining J$20.00. Thank you."


DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- One job per card: each short toll is one card with one clear next step.
- The three steps are always in the same order on every card, numbered in small grey circles: "1 Copy the message", "2 Open Uber and paste it on this trip", "3 Mark as sent".
- The amount still owed is the biggest number on the card; the toll and what Uber paid back sit under it in smaller text.
- The days left are always visible; the chip turns red with 2 days or fewer left.
- Every card says in one plain sentence what happens if the driver does nothing (see the screens for the exact wording). Never threatening.
- After "Mark as sent" the card moves to "Sent · waiting on Uber" and the driver has nothing else to do. The driver never links refunds or closes disputes: the fleet does that.
- No J$ totals for the whole fleet, no other drivers, no fleet prices, no internal words like "dispute id", "payback" or "allocation".
- Calm, short, friendly wording. Large tap targets for one-handed use.
- One view per screen.

INTERACTIONS:
- The Home card opens the "Tolls to dispute" list; a list card opens that toll's page.
- "Copy text" copies the full message and shows "Copied" with a tick for 2 seconds and the toast.
- "Open Uber App" opens the Uber Driver app. The driver finds the trip there and pastes; Roam cannot open the trip's help page directly.
- "Mark as sent" opens the bottom sheet; "Yes, I sent it" saves it, closes the sheet and shows Screen 6; the list and the Home card counts go down by one at once.
- If the 9 days run out while the page is open, the next tap on "Mark as sent" shows Screen 8's message instead of saving.
- Pull down on the list to refresh.
- A new toll to dispute shows the red dot on the bell and the Home card; no text messages are sent.
- The keyboard and screen reader read each card as one sentence: "Portmore toll, 9 Nov 7:15 AM, J$20.00 still owed, 8 of 9 days left, not sent yet."
