==================== FIRST PASTE ====================

Roam Driver - Tolls to dispute with Uber (Phone App, 390px, for drivers of fleet cars):

Design high-fidelity phone screens for a new "Tolls to dispute" part of the Roam Driver app. When a driver goes through a toll during an Uber trip, the passenger owes the toll and Uber pays it back to the driver. Sometimes Uber pays back less than the toll cost (the Portmore toll costs J$390.00 and Uber paid back J$370.00, so J$20.00 is still owed). Uber only lets the driver ask for the rest from inside the Uber driver app, within 9 days of the trip. Roam spots each short toll by itself and shows the driver everything needed: when and where the toll was, how much is still owed, and a ready-made message to paste into Uber. The driver copies the message, opens Uber, pastes it on the trip, then comes back and taps "Mark as sent". The tone is friendly, short and reassuring, like a helpful reminder, never a telling-off. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best consumer banking apps. Every screen is tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling. Draw only the screens asked for in this message.

  - top bar: menu button on the left, "Roam" in the middle, bell and profile on the right
  - bottom tab bar as in the app

  - Primary: Emerald 600 (#059669) — primary buttons, ticks, the active tab
  - Background: Slate 100 (#f1f5f9)

  - Muted text: Slate 600 (#475569)

  - Sent, waiting on Uber: Blue 700 (#1d4ed8) on #eff6ff

- DARK MODE:

- Shapes: cards 16px corner radius, buttons 12px, chips fully rounded.
- Typography: Inter. Card title 17px semibold, body 15px, helper text 13px. Money uses tabular figures, always "J$" with 2 decimals (J$20.00).
- The car plate is always shown in a small grey rounded box: "5179KZ".
- Dates and times in plain words: "9 Nov, 7:15 AM", "by Wed 18 Nov". Never "2026-11-09", never codes or ids.
- Status chips (small, fully rounded, always words): "Not sent yet" amber; "Sent · waiting on Uber" blue; "1 day left" and "Time is up" red.
- Buttons always look like buttons:
  - Primary = solid emerald with white text.
  - Secondary = white with a visible #cbd5e1 border and dark text.
  - Never underlined text links, never grey text-only buttons. Confirms are in-app bottom sheets, never browser pop-ups.
- The pieces used across these screens (draw them the same way every time):
  - Toll card in a list: line 1 time · place and the plate box; line 2 the amount still owed, big and amber; line 3 the state chip and days left with a small clock icon; a chevron on the right.

  - Numbered step card: a small grey number circle, the step title, one helper line, one button.
  - Message box: a soft grey (#f8fafc) rounded box with the full message in 15px text, never cut off.
- No gradients, glassmorphism or 3D. No AI badges.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- One job per toll: one clear next step.
- The amount still owed is always the biggest number; the toll and what Uber paid back sit under it in smaller text.
- The days left are always visible; they turn red with 2 days or fewer left.
- No totals for the whole fleet, no other drivers, no fleet prices, no internal words like "dispute id", "payback" or "allocation".
- Calm, short, friendly wording. Large tap targets for one-handed use.
- One view per screen.

==================== FOR CLAUDE — DO NOT PASTE ====================

ADDITIONAL UI COMPONENTS:
- Toll card (list), toll page header card, numbered step card, message box: as in BRAND IDENTITY.
- Consequence sentence: muted 13px text with an info icon; blue info box variant when the fleet pays (dispute opened late).
- Bottom sheet: grab handle, title, summary line, body, primary and secondary buttons stacked full width.
- Toast: dark, rounded, bottom of the screen, 2 seconds.
- Home card: amber left edge, title, one line, small red chip, full-width primary button.
- Copy button states: "Copy text" → "Copied" with a tick for 2 seconds.

INTERACTIONS:
- Lives in two places: the Home card, and the "Tolls to dispute" page opened from that card or from the menu under "My Expenses".
- The Home card opens the list; a list card opens that toll's page.
- "Copy text" copies the full message, shows "Copied" with a tick for 2 seconds and the toast.
- "Open Uber App" opens the Uber Driver app (uberdriver://). Roam cannot open the trip's help page directly; the driver finds the trip and pastes.
- "Mark as sent" opens the sheet; "Yes, I sent it" sends POST /toll/v2/driver/disputes/:id/sent and shows the sent page; the list and Home card counts drop by one at once.
- After the deadline, before the nightly job, the server answers window_closed: show the "Time is up" page instead of saving. Mark as sent is hidden when the row says the window is closed.
- The consequence sentence comes from the dispute row: driver bears it ("will be charged to you") unless the dispute opened late or the company's expired bearer is the fleet (blue "the fleet pays it, not you" box).
- Pull down on the list to refresh. A new dispute shows the red dot on the bell and the Home card; no SMS.
- Screen readers read each card as one sentence: "Portmore toll, 9 Nov 7:15 AM, J$20.00 still owed, 8 of 9 days left, not sent yet."
- The driver never links refunds or closes disputes.
