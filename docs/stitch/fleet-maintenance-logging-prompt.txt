ROAM FLEET — MAINTENANCE LOGGING FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Maintenance Logging (Desktop Web App, for fleet managers and their staff):

Design high-fidelity desktop screens for how maintenance gets logged in Fleet maintenance in the Roam Fleet app. There is no telematics: people are the only source of mileage. A fleet manager or staff member opens a car, says when the work was done and at what odometer reading, picks what was done, adds costs, and saves once. The app then works out what each part of the car is due for next. Every night at 2:00 AM a check runs and flags anything overdue or coming up. The screens must make the odometer impossible to skip and easy to trust. They must show clearly when a reading looks wrong (without blocking it) and show what is due per part of the car, not one vague number per car. The tone is calm, plain and exact: a service desk logbook, not a mechanic's spreadsheet.

BRAND IDENTITY:
- Match the existing Roam Fleet web app:
  - black top bar with the "Roam Fleet" app tile, then a white nav row: Dashboard, Fleet Operations, Driver Operations, Money, Vehicle Operations (active, underlined), Delivery Operations, Analytics, Reports, Business Finance, and the avatar "JD"
  - dark sub-bar with "Switch vehicle or search hub..." on the left and "08:47 PM EST · America/Jamaica · JM (JMD)" on the right
  - white page, centred content (max ~1400px), shadcn-style components
  - page title "Fleet maintenance" with an orange wrench icon
- LIGHT MODE (default):
  - Primary: Near-black (#030213) — primary buttons, the active tab, the selected choice outline
  - Background: White (#FFFFFF); page wash: Slate 50 (#f8fafc)
  - Surface cards: White, with a 1px border rgba(0,0,0,0.1)
  - Muted text: #717182
  - Text primary: #0a0a0a
  - Success / OK / done: Emerald (#059669)
  - Warning / due soon / check reading: Amber (#d97706)
  - Error / overdue: Red (#d4183d)
  - Neutral / odometer needed: Slate 500 (#64748b)
  - Accent: Orange (#f97316), used only for the page icon
  - Icon tiles in the log dialog: Indigo 600 (#4f46e5) line icon on a Slate 100 (#f1f5f9) rounded square
  - Muted fill for tabs and chips: Slate 100 (#f1f5f9)
- DARK MODE:
  - Background: #252525
  - Surface: #2e2e2e
  - Border: #444444
  - Text primary: #fafafa
  - Muted text: #b4b4b4
  - Primary: #fafafa (buttons become light with dark text)
  - Success: #34d399
  - Warning: #f59e0b
  - Error: #f87171
  - Neutral: #94a3b8
- Status pills (rounded, small, word + reason, never colour alone):
  - "Overdue · Oil & filter" — red fill, white text
  - "Due soon · Brake pads (front)" — amber outline, amber text
  - "OK" — emerald text, no fill
  - "Odometer needed" — slate fill, dark text
- Reading chips:
  - "Check reading" — amber outline with a small warning triangle
  - Source tag in grey: "fuel", "check-in", "service", "manual"
- Money is Jamaican dollars: "J$ 16,800"
- Kilometres always as "195,192 km"; dates as "31 Oct 2026"
- No gradients, glassmorphism or 3D. No AI badges, no confidence percentages.
- Typography: Inter. Plates, odometer readings, km, prices and counts use a mono font with tabular numerals.
- Jamaica context:
  - fleet cars: "Honda Fit 2018 · 1505LM" (no odometer reading yet), "Toyota Roomy 2019 · 5179KZ" (195,192 km, overdue)
  - service providers: "Whole-Heated Car Service LTD", "Kingston Auto Care"

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first (1440px wide), with a phone version
- The odometer comes first: every log starts with "When and at what mileage?" before any work is picked
- The car being logged is always visible: the dialog header shows the name, plate and current odometer on every step
- Capture, never block: an odd reading is saved and labelled "Check reading", never refused
- Due state is per part of the car (oil, front brake pads, each tyre), never one number per car
- Plain words first: "Next due: Oil & filter — overdue 67,192 km", never "Next due (km) 128,000"
- No internal ids, codes or row counts anywhere ("TPL 5EFCDC04" and "Schedule rows" are gone)
- One verb for one action: "Log service" on the button, the dialog title and the save button
- One save: the save button shows a spinner and cannot be pressed twice
- Every number says where it came from and how old it is: "195,192 km · 2 h ago · fuel"
- Light theme default, dark mode variant
- 8px spacing grid, 12px card corners, hairline borders, soft shadows, skeleton loading

INTERACTIONS:
- "Log service" in the page header opens Step 0 (which car?); the row "Log service" skips Step 0
- "Log this" in the due-items drawer opens the dialog on Step 1 with that item already ticked for Step 2
- Step 1 can't continue without an odometer reading above 0
- The odometer is prefilled with the latest trusted reading; changing the date re-checks the reading against that day
- A reading that doesn't fit shows the amber "Check reading" box; "Keep it and continue" carries on, and the saved reading gets the label
- Search in Step 2 jumps straight to the line form for the chosen component
- Package and quick-job cards show what is due for this car right now
- Review shows date and odometer read-only; "Edit" goes back to Step 1 and keeps every line
- "Log service" on Review saves once; the button locks with a spinner until the server answers; a retry after an error never logs twice
- After saving, a toast "Service logged · 5179KZ"; the Overview row updates its status, next due and last service without a page reload
- "Log odometer" saves a reading; a car showing "Odometer needed" switches to its real status straight away
- In Odometer history, "It's correct" clears the label; "Void" asks for a reason, then removes the reading from use
- The Overview tab sorts most urgent first; clicking a column header re-sorts
- "Run now" on the missed-check bar runs the nightly check and refreshes the page
- The Find parts tab is unchanged
