ROAM DOMINION — MAINTENANCE TEMPLATES: 2 SCREENS TO REDRAW (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Dominion - Maintenance templates, redraw (Desktop Admin, for the owner and catalog staff):

Redraw two screens of the Maintenance templates section of the Roam Dominion admin console. The other 11 screens of this section are already designed (Part schedules home, Service packages tab, one part's "who gets what" ladder, Add/edit schedule drawer, Before-saving dialog, Review list, Preview a car, History, Service schedules health card, States, Phone) — these two must look like the same product and sit beside them. In this section a PART SCHEDULE decides how often a part is due (e.g. brake fluid every 40,000 km or 24 months); a SERVICE PACKAGE is one garage visit that only lists which parts are done together and never sets how often. A PART TYPE is the list of all 49 kinds of part (Engine oil, Brake fluid, Air filter…). Part types are never deleted while anything uses them — they are retired. Dominion never shows customer names, fleet names or plates, only counts. Draw each screen at FULL HEIGHT so every element is visible in the image; nothing may be cut off, blurred or hidden behind another layer except where a frame below says so.
