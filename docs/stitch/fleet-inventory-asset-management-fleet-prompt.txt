ROAM FLEET — INVENTORY & ASSET MANAGEMENT FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Inventory & Asset Management (Desktop Web App, for fleet owners, fleet managers and their staff):

Design high-fidelity desktop screens for the Inventory & Asset Management section of the Roam Fleet app. A fleet keeps a store of equipment (spare tyres, jacks, lug wrenches, tyre pumps, phone holders, first-aid kits, warning triangles, fire extinguishers, dash cameras) and puts a standard kit into every car. The section answers four questions at a glance: what do we have and where is it, does every car have its full kit, which expensive devices need attention, and what do we need to buy. Stock never changes by typing over a number: it only changes when someone receives, issues, returns, moves, counts or writes off an item, and every change is kept in a history that cannot be edited. Expensive devices like dash cameras are tracked one by one by serial number, with warranty and inspection dates. Drivers report damaged or missing items from the driver app (designed in a separate prompt); their reports arrive here as alerts. The tone is calm, plain and exact: a well-run storeroom logbook, not an accounting system.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first (1440px wide), with a phone version
- Keep what works: the existing tab bar, the black primary button in the top-right, and the Stock table layout stay recognisable; the "Bulk Assignment" button becomes "Issue" in the same spot
- Stock is never typed over: quantities are read-only everywhere; every change goes through Receive, Issue, Return, Transfer, Count or Write-off
- Every number can be clicked to see the history behind it
- Preview before anything changes: every action dialog shows a "What will change" box before the confirm button
- Kits are the heart of the page: for each car, what it should have, what it has, what's missing
- Plain words first: "Missing 3 items — Tyre pump, First-aid kit, Warning triangle", never "compliance 0.7"
- No internal ids, UUIDs or table names anywhere
- One verb per action: "Issue" on the button, the dialog title and the confirm button
- One save: confirm buttons show a spinner and cannot be pressed twice
- Corrections never delete: a wrong movement is undone with a "Reverse" that shows in the history
- Light theme default, dark mode variant
- 8px spacing grid, 12px card corners, hairline borders, soft shadows, skeleton loading

INTERACTIONS:
- "Issue" in the header opens Screen 5; a red kit cell or "Issue missing" opens it prefilled for that car and item
- "Use" on a kit template opens Issue with that kit already chosen
- The Issue "Check" step recalculates live as cars are ticked; the confirm button shows the exact count it will issue
- Quantities are never editable; the pencil on an item edits details only
- "Archive" is disabled while an item still has stock, with a tooltip saying why
- "Reverse" on a movement asks for a reason and adds a REVERSAL line; nothing is deleted
- Write-offs and count changes above J$ 20,000.00 go to "Needs approval"; a second person approves from Alerts
- Receiving a tracked item requires one serial per unit; "Scan" fills the serial from the camera
- Saving a new kit version asks whether to move cars to it
- Every confirm button locks with a spinner until the server answers; a retry never saves twice
- After any action, KPI tiles, the Stock row, the kit matrix and Alerts update without a page reload
- Clicking any quantity or value opens Movements filtered to that item and location
- "Create draft order" from Suggested or from the Issue shortfall opens a prefilled PO in Draft
- A driver report (sent from the driver app) appears in Alerts with its photo and a "Review" button
- Toll tags and fuel cards are shown as "Linked" and open their own pages; they can't be issued here
