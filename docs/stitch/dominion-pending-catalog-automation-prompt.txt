Roam Dominion - Pending Catalog with automated research (Desktop Admin, for the owner):

Design high-fidelity desktop screens that upgrade "Pending catalog" in the Roam Dominion admin console from a manual to-do list into an automated research queue. When a fleet asks for a part (or a car) that Roam doesn't have, Roam now researches it on its own, one request at a time in the order they arrived. It looks the part up on a short list of trusted parts catalogues, checks each page really shows that part number for that exact car, and then either adds the part by itself (when two trusted catalogues agree) or puts it in front of the owner to review. The owner's job becomes: glance at the queue, approve or fix what needs review, and undo anything that looks wrong. Every result shows where it came from. Dominion is the master reference database, so these screens never show customer names, company names or plates: requests are grouped by car version and part, with a count of how many fleets asked. Find parts is information only, so there are no prices, suppliers or stock anywhere. The tone is a calm, trustworthy control room.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first (1440px wide)

- Never show who asked; show how many fleets asked

INTERACTIONS:
- Clicking a row opens the drawer; the table keeps its filter and scroll
- "Approve and add" adds the part, marks every request in the group Added, tells every fleet that asked, and closes the drawer
- "Edit and add" turns the candidate into a form; a source URL is required
- "Undo" asks once ("Undo this add?") and then reopens the request as Needs review
- "Resume", the switches and "Retry" act at once and show a small toast
- The research strip and the researching row update on their own every 10 seconds
- Hover: rows highlight in the primary container colour
