ROAM FLEET — FIND PARTS · YOUR REQUESTS AND "NOW ON FILE" — FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Find Parts, request tracking (Desktop Web App, for fleet managers and their staff):

Design high-fidelity screens that update how fleet managers follow the parts they asked Roam to add from the Find parts tab in Fleet maintenance. Roam now researches each request automatically, so a request moves on its own from "Sent" to "Roam is looking" to "Added", usually within a day. People should always know where their request is, get told plainly when a part is now on file, and be able to open it in one click. Find parts is an information tool: it tells people which parts fit their car. It never shows prices, suppliers, stock or quotes. The tone is calm and reassuring: "we're on it, and here's the answer".

  - the white card heading "Fleet Maintenance" with the orange wrench icon, and the tabs on the right: Overview (badge 2), Service history, Find parts (active)

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first (1440px wide), with one phone screen
- Every request shows where it is right now and what happens next
- When a part is added, the person is told once, plainly, with one button to see it
- "Couldn't find" is never a dead end: it says why and when Roam tries again
- All navigation stays in the sticky header; no bottom buttons

SCREEN 1: YOUR REQUESTS (WITH TIMELINE)
- Fleet frame; the sticky header shows "← Back to Find parts" and the title "Your requests"
- Filter chips: "All (4)", "Waiting (2)", "Added (1)", "Couldn't find (1)"
- Request cards, newest first, each with:
  - part and side, car and version, plate in mono, "Asked 01 Nov 2026"
  - status chip on the right
  - a slim horizontal timeline of four dots: "Sent → Roam is looking → Checked → Added", with done dots filled and the current one ringed; time under each done dot in mono
  - Added: the part line "Toyota 90916-02729 · Drive belt" and a primary button "See the part"
  - Roam is looking: muted line "Roam is checking trusted parts catalogues. Usually within a day."
  - Couldn't find: the reason line and "We'll try again on 8 Nov.", plus a text link "Add a note or photo" that opens the note field
- Empty state: "No requests yet. When a part isn't on file, ask Roam from the search."

SCREEN 2: "NOW ON FILE" NOTICE AND RESULTS
- Two frames side by side
- Frame A — the "What part do you need?" page with a notice bar directly under the sticky header:
  - emerald left edge, tick icon, "Drive belt for your Toyota Roomy 2019 is now on file."
  - buttons "See the part" (primary) and "Dismiss" (text); when there are several: "3 parts you asked for are now on file" with "See them"
  - the "Belts & hoses" area card now shows "1 part on file for your car"
- Frame B — Results for Drive belt:
  - heading "Drive belt for your Toyota Roomy 2019", sub "Pre-Facelift · 1KR-FE · 2WD · chassis M900A"
  - Best match card "Toyota 90916-02729 — V-ribbed belt", chip "Checked against 2 catalogues", buttons "Details" and "Use in a service log"
  - "Details" expands to show "Fits: M900A · 1KR-FE · 2016–2020" and "Added 01 Nov 2026 after your request"
  - the folded line at the end for the other match types, with "Ask Roam to find another option"

SCREEN 3: REQUEST SENT AND DAILY LIMIT
- Two frames of the "Not on file yet" request screen, after pressing "Ask Roam to add this part"
- Frame A — sent:
  - green tick, "Request sent"
  - "Roam is checking trusted parts catalogues for your exact car. Usually within a day."
  - when another fleet already asked: "Others asked for this too, so it's already in line."
  - buttons "View your requests" and text link "Search a different part"
- Frame B — limit reached:
  - calm amber card, "You've sent 30 requests today. Roam will pick up more tomorrow."
  - the form stays filled in, the send button is disabled with "Try again tomorrow"

SCREEN 4: PHONE (390px) — YOUR REQUESTS AND NOTICE
- Two phone frames side by side
- The sticky header is the compact bar: "←", "Your requests"
- Frame 1: request cards full width, status chip under the title, the timeline as four small dots with labels below
- Frame 2: the "now on file" notice as a top banner over the What part? page, with "See the part"

ADDITIONAL UI COMPONENTS:
- Request card with four-step timeline
- Status chips: Sent, Roam is looking, Added, Couldn't find
- "Now on file" notice bar (single and grouped)
- "Checked against 2 catalogues" chip
- Daily limit card

INTERACTIONS:
- Request status updates on its own when the page is open (every 30 seconds) and when the person comes back to the tab
- "See the part" opens Results for that car and part, with the right side already picked
- "Dismiss" hides the notice for good for that part
- "Add a note or photo" on a Couldn't find card sends the note to Roam and the card goes back to "Sent"
- Hover: cards lift slightly; focus rings are 2px near-black
