Roam Dominion - Part pictures (desktop admin console for the owner, who is not a mechanic):

Design high-fidelity, modern screens that add realistic part pictures to the Roam Dominion admin console. Today the owner uploads a page they saved from a catalogue website, and Roam reads every part number on it. Now, when the page is saved as one single file, Roam also takes the maker's line drawing from inside it and makes a realistic studio photo of each part — the air filter, a shock absorber, a brake pad — so anyone looking up that part can see what it looks like. The pictures are made by AI a minute or two after the upload, stored once per part, and reused for every car that uses that part. The owner can look at each picture, remake it, or hide it from Roam Fleet with one click, and can edit the short "materials" description Roam uses for each group of parts (brakes, filters, suspension…). It must feel effortless and premium, like a modern photo library: big clear pictures, calm states, one obvious next step. Make each screen tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling and no cut-off boxes. Draw only the screens asked for in this message. Dominion is the master reference database: no fleet names, plates, drivers, vehicle counts, "used by", prices, suppliers, stock or quantities anywhere.
