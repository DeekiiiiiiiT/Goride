==================== FIRST PASTE ====================

Roam Fleet - Maintenance: due list, filling in service history, and work orders (Desktop Web App, for fleet owners, fleet managers and their staff):

Design high-fidelity, modern screens for the Maintenance area of Roam Fleet. Every car has a list of parts that come due by km or by months, whichever comes first. Each due line says in plain words where its schedule comes from ("Car maker · owner's manual" or "Roam policy") and where the count started ("Counted from your service on 22 Aug 2026 at 180,088 km"). When nobody knows when a part was last done, the line says "History unknown" and asks to check it at the next service. It is never shown as overdue, never as OK. A short one-time "Fill in service history" step lets the owner type the last km and date for each part, or tick "Check at next service". Any due line can be logged on the spot with "Log this". A new "Work orders" tab shows every car that is at the garage or waiting to go, with the jobs on it. Adding a car now asks for its current odometer, and marks it "Hard use" (taxi, heavy traffic) from the start. The person using this is not a mechanic: every card says in plain words what it is for. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best consumer car-care apps. Every screen is tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling. Draw only the screens asked for in this message.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Each screen answers "what needs me now?" first: overdue, then due soon, then history unknown, then OK.
- One big plain number per line. The km and date maths is never shown as a formula.
- "History unknown" is calm, never alarming: a question to answer, not a warning.
- Short plain words. Never explain how Roam works inside. Never say what Roam's database has or doesn't have.
- One primary button per screen or dialog.
- One view per screen.

INTERACTIONS:
- "Log this" opens the Log service wizard at step 2 with that part already picked and the car chosen.
- "Fill in history" / "Fill in" open S2 for that car; Save writes last_performed_km/date per part (source 'manual'); ticked rows stay History unknown. Recompute runs at once; the drawer updates.
- Overview "To do" card shows only while there is something to do: cars with no reading (MER-13 alert), cars with History unknown parts. Hidden when empty.
- Work orders tab: list open first; "Mark done" opens the Log service wizard prefilled with the jobs (one service per work order). "Cancel" → in-app confirm with reason (min 10 characters). Requests tab approve opens or creates the work order it belongs to (MER-15).
- Do not drive switch on the work order sets/clears fleet.vehicles.do_not_drive with reason; clearing uses public.fleet_vehicles (MER-09). Driver is notified.
- Add a car (S6): odometer required, saved as first reading source 'manual' (MER-26); Hard use is pre-selected (MER-25).
- Turn off hard use (S7): PATCH severe_duty false with the typed reason (min 10 characters); writes fleet.maintenance_audit_log (who, when, why). Turning it on needs no reason.
- "Why this schedule?" (S8): rule source, manual page, normal vs hard use numbers, counted-from, next due.
- Owner/manager only: Turn off hard use, Cancel work order, Do not drive. Staff can log, fill history and add jobs.
