Roam Fleet - Fuel fills from drivers' phones (Desktop Web App, for the fleet owner and fleet manager):

Design screens for how Roam Fleet shows fuel fills that drivers log from their phones. Drivers now log a fill by taking two photos (the odometer, then the pump or receipt) and tapping Save, even with no signal. The fill waits on the driver's phone and sends by itself when the signal comes back. Then Roam reads the total, litres and odometer from the photos. The owner needs to see two new things. First, which drivers still have fills waiting on their phone, and since when ("Kenny — 2 fills on his phone since 7:03 AM"). Second, in the Fuel Review Queue, the fills whose photos couldn't be read, with both photos side by side, what was read, what's missing, and a way to type in the missing numbers before approving. The person using this is not an accountant: every card says in plain words what it is for. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best business banking apps. Every screen is tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling. Draw only the screens asked for in this message.

- A missing value is labelled, never hidden and never shown as J$0.00.

- Plain words only: no "outbox", "queue item", "sync", "capture id", "OCR".
- One view per screen.

INTERACTIONS:
- Spec: docs/fuel-log-offline-first-audit.md (S1, S2, S6, S7, FO-14; section 5 "Roam Fleet — owner side").
- The waiting-on-phone card and the driver chip read GET /fuel/phone-outbox (S2). Hidden when every count is 0.
- "App 1.0.30" is app_version from the phone's report. "Phone last seen" is last_seen_at.
- Review Queue "Needs a look" rows are fills with photoReadStatus = needs_look (S6) or no station after S7. The reason chip comes from the server reason in plain words.
- "From photo" = value set by the photo reader; "From location" = station matched from the phone's GPS; "Typed by you" = entered in this panel.
- Save numbers stores total and litres on the same fill and re-runs the fuel engine. Save and approve also runs the existing approve. Reject uses the existing reject (with its reason dialog).
- Use this station sets the station on the fill (existing approve station link).
- The Roam Fleet driver section (apps/fleet/src/components/driver-portal) gets the driver fuel screens from docs/stitch/fuel-log-offline-driver-prompt.txt through the shared package (FO-16). They aren't redrawn here.
