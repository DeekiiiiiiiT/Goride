Roam Fleet - Week Result in Week Reconciliation (Desktop Web App with a phone layout, for the fleet owner):

Design the page the fleet owner sees after a week is closed in "Week Reconciliation" inside Roam Fleet. Closing a week means every upload, toll, fuel fill, trip and driver payment for one Monday-to-Sunday week has been checked and settled. Once that is done, the owner does not want to see the checks again. They want one answer: how did the business do this week? The page shows exactly five numbers and nothing else: Revenue, Expenses and Profit for the week, then how that profit was shared between the fleet and the drivers. Revenue is everything the cars earned (fares and tips the fleet kept). Expenses are what it cost to run the cars (fuel, tolls after platforms paid them back, repairs and fees logged for that week). Profit is Revenue minus Expenses, before anyone is paid. Fleet profit is what the fleet keeps after paying the drivers. Driver profit is the drivers' cut after expenses, added up for every driver who drove that week (a fleet can have one driver or fifty; the page never lists drivers by name). Fleet profit plus Driver profit always equals Profit. The person using this is not an accountant: big clear numbers, short plain labels, nothing to click except "Reopen week". The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best business banking apps. Every screen is tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Five numbers, nothing else. No upload list, no evidence grid, no history, no step details, no tables, no charts besides the one split bar.
- The numbers always add up and the page shows it: Revenue − Expenses = Profit, and Fleet profit + Driver profit = Profit.
- Big and readable from across a desk: the three top numbers are the largest things on the page.
- Words, not jargon: "Revenue", "Expenses", "Profit", "Fleet profit", "Driver profit". Never "net", "gross", "settlement", "ledger" or "share cash".
- Green means the week made money; red only when Profit is below zero. Nothing else is coloured.
- The only action is "Reopen week".
- One view per screen.

- Closed banner: dark bar, white text, padlock icon, red-outline Reopen button on the right.


INTERACTIONS:
- Opening a closed week goes straight to this page.
- Hovering a number shows the exact amount with 2 decimals.
- "Reopen week" opens the dialog in Screen 4; confirming reopens the week and takes the person back to the step they need.
- While the numbers are still being worked out, the page shows Screen 3 and fills in by itself when they are ready; no reload button.
- On the phone, the cards stack in the same order and nothing is hidden.
