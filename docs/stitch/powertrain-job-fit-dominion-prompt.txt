ROAM DOMINION — VEHICLES: POWERTRAIN + JOB FIT FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Dominion - Vehicles · Powertrain and job fit (Desktop Admin, for the owner):

Design modern, premium screens that let the owner say what powers each car model and which maintenance jobs fit which cars. They sit inside the existing Roam Dominion admin console (Vehicles section) and must feel like the same product. Today every car is offered every maintenance job, so an electric car is offered an oil change and a petrol car is offered high-voltage battery work. Each car model now gets a POWERTRAIN (Petrol / diesel engine, Mild hybrid, Hybrid, Plug-in hybrid, Range-extender, Electric, Hydrogen fuel cell) and a FUEL (Petrol, Diesel, Natural gas, Hydrogen, Electric). Roam works it out from the car's existing data and shows where it came from ("Worked out from 'Hybrid(petrol) / PHEV'"). The owner confirms it, or fixes the few that couldn't be worked out, in a "Powertrain to check" list (12 models today). Each maintenance JOB, and each job GROUP (Engine, Exhaust & emissions, Hybrid & electric…), says which cars it FITS, using simple presets (All, Combustion, Electrified, Plug-in, Spark-ignition) instead of lists of codes. A job can follow its group ("Same as group") or set its own. A job can also be marked "High-voltage work", which will later require a safety lockout checklist. Nothing is ever deleted, and old services are never changed. Dominion only sets the rules; it never shows fleet names, plates or prices. The app should feel like a polished consumer product in the style of Linear, Stripe and Airbnb: friendly, airy and easy to scan. Each screen is one single view, light mode only (dark mode comes from the Stitch theme switch). Make every screen tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling and nothing cut off.

BRAND IDENTITY:
- Match the existing Dominion console:
  - a dark navy left sidebar with "Dominion · Roam Admin Console"; groups MASTER DATA (Overview, Vehicles — highlighted, Places and Maps, Fuel, Tolls, Tax and Vendors), ENGINES, DISTRIBUTION, PLATFORM, BACK OFFICE
  - top bar: search "Search entities, engines, routes…" with a ⌘K chip, moon icon, user "DT · Deeki T · Owner"
  - breadcrumb "Dominion › Master data › Vehicles" in small soft-grey text
- LIGHT MODE (default):
  - Primary: #0153dc; primary container: #dbe1ff
  - Success: #006e31; warning: #8a5a00; error: #a83836
  - Outline: #797a84
  - Background: #faf8fe; cards white
  - Text: #30323b; helper text #5d5f68
- DARK MODE (for reference; draw light only):
  - Background: #0b1220; cards #111a2e; text #e2e8f0; primary #5b8def; success #4ade80; warning #fbbf24; error #f87171
- Shapes: cards and drawers 16px rounded corners; buttons and inputs 10px; chips are fully rounded pills
- Depth: soft, light shadows on cards and drawers; at most a very light 1px border
- Typography: Inter. Titles 22–26px semibold, body 15px, helper text 13px. Sentence case everywhere. Numbers use lined-up (tabular) figures.
- Icons: Material Symbols Outlined, on soft-tinted round tiles
- Powertrain chips, each an icon plus a word (never colour alone):
  - "Petrol / diesel engine": soft grey, engine icon
  - "Mild hybrid": soft grey, leaf icon
  - "Hybrid": soft teal, battery-charging icon
  - "Plug-in hybrid": soft teal, plug icon
  - "Range-extender": soft teal, route icon
  - "Electric": soft green, bolt icon
  - "Hydrogen fuel cell": soft blue, water-drop icon
- Status chips: "Worked out" soft grey with a sparkle icon · "Confirmed" soft green with a check · "Needs checking" soft amber with a question icon
- "High-voltage work" chip: soft amber with a lightning icon

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Modern and friendly: generous white space (24px inside cards, 32px between sections), large clear headings, nothing cramped
- Plain words for a normal person: "What powers this car", "Fits", "Same as group", "Fits 2,075 of 2,679 car models". No codes, no arrays, no database words
- Always show where a value came from ("Worked out from …", "Confirmed by Deeki T on 15 Nov 2026")
- Presets first; "Custom…" only when needed
- Every action is a real button with a fill or a visible outline, never a text link. One solid blue primary button per view. Cancel is red (red outline, red text)
- No browser pop-ups; every message is inside the app
- Desktop-first (1440px)

INTERACTIONS:
- "Confirm" on a model sets the status to Confirmed and shows "Confirmed by … on …"; on the check list the row fades out and the count goes down
- "Edit" switches the card to selects; "Save and confirm" saves and confirms; "Cancel" restores the old values
- A model whose car data changes after it was confirmed shows Screen 4 until it's confirmed again
- In a job drawer, switching to "Set for this job" reveals the preset select; "Custom…" reveals the checkbox chips; switching back to "Same as group" hides them and shows the group's value
- The "Fits N of 2,679" line updates as choices change, before saving
- Turning on "High-voltage work" adds the amber chip to the job's header
- "See which" opens a simple list of the matching car models
- Save and Cancel are real buttons; Esc closes the drawer; focus returns to the row that opened it; everything works by keyboard
