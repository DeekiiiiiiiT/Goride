ROAM FLEET — MAINTENANCE: WHERE EACH DUE DATE COMES FROM (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Maintenance, schedule sources and alerts (Desktop Web App, for fleet owners, fleet managers and their staff):

Design the high-fidelity screens that change in Roam Fleet's Maintenance area now that every service schedule comes from Roam's master database (Roam Dominion). The existing Maintenance screens (Overview tab, Due items drawer for one car, Log service steps, Log odometer, Service history) are already designed; these screens sit beside them and must look like the same product. What's new for the fleet manager: every due line now says where its schedule came from ("Car maker", "Part maker" or "Roam policy"); a line can be marked "Needs checking" when Roam hasn't confirmed it yet; when Roam corrects a schedule, the line shows "Updated by Roam" with the date; a car with no odometer reading yet shows "Waiting for odometer" instead of an invented due distance; a fleet can set its own tighter interval for one part on one car ("Custom schedule", with a reason); and a car can be marked "Hard use" (taxi, delivery, heavy traffic) to use shorter severe-duty schedules. Alerts change too: one alert per part per car that stays open until the work is logged, instead of a new alert every day. The tone is calm, plain and exact: a service desk logbook.

- A reading lower than the last one is saved but flagged "Looks wrong — lower than the last reading" and is not used for due dates
