==================== FIRST PASTE ====================

Roam Fleet - Fill details panel and Decide pay window (Desktop Web App, for the fleet owner and fleet manager):

Design two in-app windows that open from the Transaction Logs page of Roam Fleet. The first is a side panel that shows one saved fuel fill in full when the owner taps a row: how much, how many litres, where, the odometer, how it was paid and logged, and a button to change it. The second is a centred window where the owner decides what to pay a driver when the gas card statement and the pump total don't agree. The person using this is not an accountant: every label is in plain words. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best business banking apps, with generous spacing, soft shadows and large clear type. Every screen is tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling. Draw only the screens asked for in this message.


BRAND IDENTITY:
- Keep the existing Roam Fleet app frame exactly as it is today (do not redesign it):
  - Top: a white nav row with the black "Roam Fleet" app tile, then: Dashboard, Fleet Operations (active, underlined), Driver Operations, Money, Vehicle Operations, Delivery Operations, Analytics, Reports, Business Finance.
  - Dark bar below it: a dark navy strip with a search box "Switch vehicle or search hub…" on the left, and on the right "03:11:28 PM EST" and "America/Jamaica".
- Theme: "Calm finance desk" — clear, trustworthy, quiet and modern. Not a spreadsheet, not a developer tool.
- LIGHT MODE (default):
  - Primary: near-black #000000 — primary buttons
  - Page wash: #f8f9ff; cards and panels: #FFFFFF with a soft shadow (0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.04), 0 8px 24px rgba(15,23,42,0.06))
  - Soft blue panel inside cards: #eff4ff; input fields: #eff4ff, white when focused
  - Text: #0d1c2e; muted text: #47464c

  - Problem / mismatch / pay nothing: red #ba1a1a on #ffdad6

- DARK MODE:
  - Page #1c1c1e, panels #2a2a2d, soft panel #18181b, text #fafafa, muted #a1a1aa, primary buttons white with near-black text
  - Green #34d399 on #064e3b; amber #fbbf24 on #451a03; red #f87171 on #450a0a; neutral #d4d4d8 on #27272a

- Dates in plain words: "Tue 24 Nov, 4:53 AM". Never "2026-11-24", never codes, ids or internal words.

  - Secondary = white with a visible #c9c5cd border and dark text.
  - Danger = white with a red border and red text; a final "pay nothing" confirm is solid red.
  - Never underlined text links, never icon-only buttons without a label. Confirms are in-app windows, never browser pop-ups.

Header: title, one muted line under it, a chip for how the fill was logged, and a secondary "Close" button top-right. Sections separated by 24px with a 17px section title. A bottom bar pinned to the bottom with the buttons.

  - Tank bar: a rounded bar showing how full the fill made the tank, with "38% of the tank" under it.
  - Centred window: 560px wide, 16px corners, over the dimmed page, title + one muted line, content, buttons bottom-right.
  - Choice card: a large white rounded card with a bold title and one muted line; the selected one has a near-black 2px border and a filled round dot.
- No gradients, glassmorphism or 3D. No AI badges, no sparkles.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- The money comes first: the amount is the biggest thing in the panel.
- Show only what Roam knows; a missing value says "—" or "Waiting on statement", never J$0.00.
- Calm, not alarming: red only when something needs a money decision. Waiting is amber.
- Plain words only: no "anchor", "ledger", "seal", "derived", "variance", "tolerance", "void", "entry mode".
- One view per screen.
