Roam Dominion - Maintenance · Jobs (Desktop Admin, for the owner and catalog staff):

Design four screens for the new "Jobs" tab of the Maintenance templates section of the Roam Dominion admin console. It replaces the old "Part types" tab. The other screens of this section (Part schedules, Service packages, Review list, History) already exist — these must look like the same product and sit beside them. In this section a JOB is one piece of maintenance work you can log, e.g. "Spark Plugs", "Throttle Body Cleaning", "Wheel Alignment". Every job has a HOME GROUP (Engine, Filters, Brakes, Tires & wheels, Fluids, Belts & hoses, Lighting & visibility, Suspension & steering, Transmission, HVAC, Battery & electrical) and a FORM TYPE: a SERVICE (only a cost is logged) or a PART (quantity, price, brand and part number are logged). Inside a group, jobs can be arranged into SECTION GROUPS and SECTIONS, e.g. Engine → "External bolt-on & induction" → "Air Intake & Induction". A job from another group can be LISTED in a section without moving (e.g. Engine Air Filter lives in Filters but is listed in Engine · Air Intake & Induction). "ALSO DO" links suggest work that naturally goes together (e.g. Piston Ring Replacement → Head Gasket, note "The head is already off."). Jobs are never deleted — they are retired. Dominion never shows customer names, fleet names, plates, prices, suppliers or stock. Draw each screen at FULL HEIGHT so every element is visible; nothing may be cut off, blurred or hidden behind another layer except where a frame below says so.

BUTTON RULES (strict):
- Every action is a real button with a visible border or fill — never plain text, never underlined.
- Destructive buttons (Retire, Delete, Remove, Cancel) are red (#a83836 fill or red outline).
- One primary (blue) button per screen or drawer.
- No browser pop-ups; confirmations are in-app dialogs.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Calm and exact, a well-kept service manual, not a spreadsheet
- Plain words first, codes small and muted
- No percentages, no made-up IDs, no server/node/sync/version labels, no prices, suppliers, stock, customer names or plates
- Use only the real data listed under each screen
