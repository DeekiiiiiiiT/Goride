ROAM FLEET — FIND PARTS (INFORMATION ONLY) — FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Find Parts, version 2 (Desktop Web App, for fleet managers and their staff):

Design high-fidelity desktop screens that update the "Find parts" tab inside Fleet maintenance in the Roam Fleet app. Find parts is an information tool, not a shop. A fleet manager, or a staff member who knows nothing about cars, picks their car, picks a part, says which side if it matters, and sees which part numbers fit their exact car. They buy the part wherever they like. Nothing on these screens shows prices, suppliers, stock, quotes or maintenance schedules. When Roam doesn't have a part on file for that car yet, the person asks Roam to add it with one tap, and can follow the request under "Your requests". The tone is calm, plain and helpful: a parts encyclopedia for your own cars.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first (1440px wide), with one phone screen
- One question per step, big tappable cards
- All navigation is at the top: one sticky Find parts header holds ← Back, the step trail, the car and part chips, and Next →. There are no Back / Continue / Show parts buttons at the bottom of any page.
- Browse by area first; common parts are a shortcut underneath
- Every part tile tells you up front whether Roam has it on file for this car
- "Not on file yet" is never a dead end: one tap asks Roam to add it

SCREEN 1: WHAT PART? (WITH THE NEW STICKY HEADER)
- Fleet page frame as in Brand Identity, "Find parts" tab active
- Sticky Find parts header, full width of the content, solid white, thin bottom border, flush under the dark status strip when scrolling:
  - left: round "←" button with the label "Back"
  - centre: step trail "✓ 1. Pick car / ✓ 2. Version: Pre-Facelift · 1KR-FE · 2WD / ● 3. Part / 4. Side"; done steps are clickable
  - car chip "Toyota Roomy 2019 · 5179KZ" with a pencil; version tag "PRE-FACELIFT · 1KR-FE · 2WD"
  - right: primary button "Next →", disabled until a part is chosen
- Page heading "What part do you need?", sub line "Search by name, browse by area, or describe the problem."
- Search box: "Try 'brake pads', 'o2 sensor' or a part number…", an "ESC to clear" chip, mic button
- FIRST section "By area", with "11 areas" on the right:
  - a grid of area cards, each with icon, name and "3 parts on file for your car" (or "None on file yet" in grey)
  - areas: Battery & electrical, Belts & hoses, Brakes (selected, black), Engine, Filters, Fluids, HVAC, Lighting & visibility, Suspension & steering, Tires & wheels, Transmission
- Under the selected area, the sub-part chips: "Brake pads · 6 on file", "Brake shoes / drums · 2 on file", "Brake fluid · Not on file yet", "Brake rotor · Not on file yet"
- SECOND section "Common parts", smaller, a shortcut row:
  - 8 compact tiles: Oil filter, Air filter, Brake pads, Wiper blades, Battery, Spark plugs, Cabin filter, Headlight bulb
  - each tile has a status chip: "On file" in emerald or "Not on file yet" in grey
- THIRD section "What's wrong with the car?":
  - problems: "Squeaks or grinds when I brake", "Car won't start, or clicks", "Engine light is on", "A light is out"
  - each opens a short plain answer with the likely parts
- No maintenance banner, no "services due", no prices

INTERACTIONS:
- Choosing a part:
  - parts on file and no side question → Results;
  - the part has sides (brakes, shocks, lights) → Which side?;
  - nothing on file → Not on file yet (Screen 3).
- ← Back always returns to the previous step; Next → moves on only when the step has a choice
- The header stays at the top while scrolling; nothing shows above or through it
- "Ask Roam to add this part" sends the request, shows the sent state, and the request appears in Your requests
- When Roam adds the part, the request turns "Added" and "See the part" opens the results
- Hover: cards lift slightly; selected cards get a 2px near-black outline
