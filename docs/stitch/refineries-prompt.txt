OVERALL LOOK — high fidelity, modern

A premium, modern market-data console in the Dominion design system. It should feel like Bloomberg or Stripe Sigma rebuilt with calm, airy spacing: crisp 1px borders, 12–16px corner radius, subtle layered surfaces (page background a very light cool grey, cards white with a hairline border and a very soft shadow), generous whitespace, and a strict 8px spacing grid. Typography: the Dominion sans for text, and the Dominion mono with tabular figures for every price, date and count. Large price numbers are confident and light-weight, not bold blocks. Colour is restrained: neutral greys almost everywhere, Dominion blue for selection and primary actions, a small set of muted data colours for chart lines (blue, teal, violet, amber), green only for "Current" freshness, and amber for "Due" or "Revised". Price rises and falls use ▲ / ▼ with neutral text, never alarm red. Use soft micro-details: hairline dividers, small pill chips, subtle hover lift on cards, focus rings, skeleton shimmer. Keep it real: no glassmorphism, no gradients on data, no 3D, no decorative illustrations, no "REGION" badges or "LOC:" footers.

Shell: the existing Dominion admin shell. Left sidebar is dark navy with "Fuel" selected; the top bar is white with the search field and the owner avatar "DT · Owner".
