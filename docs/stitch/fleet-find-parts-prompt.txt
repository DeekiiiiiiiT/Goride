ROAM FLEET — FIND PARTS FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Find Parts (Desktop Web App, for fleet managers and their staff):

Design high-fidelity desktop screens for a new "Find parts" tab inside Fleet maintenance in the Roam Fleet app. It sits next to the existing "By vehicle" and "Service ledger" tabs. A fleet manager, or a staff member who knows nothing about cars, picks one of their vehicles (or any make, model and year), optionally picks the part they need, and sees every part that fits, grouped into five match types, with supplier prices, what is already on their shelf, and a one-tap way to get a quote. If the part isn't on file yet, they ask Roam to find it with a request that is already filled in for them. The tone is calm, plain and helpful: a self-serve parts counter, not a mechanic's catalogue.

BRAND IDENTITY:
- Match the existing Roam Fleet web app:
  - black top bar with the "Roam Fleet" app tile, then a white nav row: Dashboard, Fleet Operations, Driver Operations, Money, Vehicle Operations (active, underlined), Delivery Operations, Analytics, Reports, Business Finance, and the avatar "JD"
  - white page, centred content (max ~1400px), shadcn-style components
  - page title "Fleet maintenance" with an orange wrench icon
- LIGHT MODE (default):
  - Primary: Near-black (#030213) — primary buttons, the active tab, the selected choice outline
  - Background: White (#FFFFFF); page wash: Slate 50 (#f8fafc)
  - Surface cards: White, with a 1px border rgba(0,0,0,0.1)
  - Muted text: #717182
  - Text primary: #0a0a0a
  - Success / healthy / in stock / verified: Emerald (#059669)
  - Warning / needs checking / old price: Amber (#d97706)
  - Error / overdue: Red (#d4183d)
  - Accent: Orange (#f97316), used only for the page icon
  - Muted fill for tabs and chips: Slate 100 (#f1f5f9)
- DARK MODE:
  - Background: #252525
  - Surface: #2e2e2e
  - Border: #444444
  - Text primary: #fafafa
  - Muted text: #b4b4b4
  - Primary: #fafafa (buttons become light with dark text)
  - Success: #34d399
  - Warning: #f59e0b
  - Error: #f87171
- Five match-type accent bars (thin 4px left edge on each result section):
  - 1 Made for your car: #2563eb
  - 2 Same car, different badge: #0e7490
  - 3 Fits your engine: #b45309
  - 4 Other brands that replace it: #7c3aed
  - 5 Fits by size: #059669
- Chips:
  - "Verified": green dot
  - "Needs checking": amber outline
  - "On your shelf — 2": emerald fill
  - "Price may be old": grey
  - Small grey tag for the technical match name ("OEM", "Sister", "Engine", "Aftermarket", "Universal")
- Money is Jamaican dollars: "J$ 4,250"; "landed" means delivered to Jamaica, including freight and duty
- No gradients, glassmorphism or 3D. No AI badges, no confidence percentages.
- Typography: Inter. Part numbers, plates, prices, counts and years use a mono font with tabular numerals.
- Jamaica context:
  - fleet cars: "Honda Fit 2018 · 1505LM", "Toyota Roomy 2019 · 5179KZ"
  - suppliers: "Kingston Auto Parts", "Mandeville Motor Spares", "JDM Direct (Japan)"
  - brands: Toyota, MANN-FILTER, NGK, Denso, NWB

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first (1440px wide), with a phone version
- The fastest path is one tap on "My vehicles"; "Another car" is always one click away
- One question per step, with big tappable cards, not dense forms
- Ask "Which one is yours?" and "Front or back?" only when the answer changes the result
- Plain words first and the trade word second: "Made for your car" with a small "OEM" tag
- The part is optional: the person can always skip to "Show everything for this car"
- A sticky trail of choices ("Honda Fit 2018 › 1.5 hybrid › Brake pads › Front") lets the person jump back to any step
- The best answer comes first: one "Best match" card with price, stock and delivery time
- Every action asks for confirmation first (quantity, urgency); nothing is sent by a single accidental click
- A missing part is never a dead end: "Ask Roam to find it" is pre-filled
- Light theme default, dark mode variant
- 8px spacing grid, 12px card corners, hairline borders, soft shadows, skeleton loading

INTERACTIONS:
- Tapping a "My vehicles" card skips straight to "What part?" (the version is already known)
- For "Another car", choosing make, model and year moves to "Which one is yours?" only when the versions give different answers
- "Not sure" shows merged results with a version chip on every row
- Search waits until typing pauses, then suggests; picking a suggestion skips to results (or to the side question)
- Area tiles and problem rows expand inline; a "Services due" chip jumps straight to that part's results
- "Front or back?" appears only for parts that come in front and back versions
- Every chip in the trail reopens its step
- The URL keeps the tab, vehicle, part and side, so the Vehicles page and "Services due" can link straight to results
- "Request a quote", "Add to stock" and "Ask Roam to find it" always open a confirmation step with quantity and urgency first
- "Use in a service log" opens the existing Log service dialog with the vehicle and part already chosen
- After any send, a toast appears and the item shows up in "Your parts activity" without reloading
- The "Compatible parts" header button switches to this tab
- The "By vehicle" and "Service ledger" tabs are unchanged
