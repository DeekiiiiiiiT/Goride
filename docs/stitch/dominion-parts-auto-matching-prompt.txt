ROAM DOMINION — PARTS AUTO-MATCHING — FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Dominion - Parts auto-matching (Desktop Admin, for the owner and catalog editors):

Design high-fidelity desktop screens that let Dominion match parts to cars on its own, the way a parts catalogue does. Today a part only fits the cars someone typed in by hand. With these screens:
- every car carries its factory part numbers;
- every part carries the factory numbers it replaces, plus its industry index numbers (WVA, FMSI);
- platforms record which parts they share with other platforms.

Dominion then works out every match itself and sorts it into the five match types: Direct OEM, Sister vehicle, Component / engine compatible, Aftermarket cross-reference and Universal. Every match says why it matches. Matches Dominion isn't sure about are never hidden or blocked: they get a "Needs checking" label, and the owner decides with Keep or Remove.

Dominion is the master reference database: it never shows fleet names or plates. Parts are information only: no prices, suppliers, stock or quantities anywhere. The tone is a calm, careful control room. Nothing changes by surprise, and every automatic decision can be explained and undone.

These screens ADD to the existing Dominion screens. They don't replace them. The vehicle specification inspector (the eye overlay in Vehicle catalog), the Full database part drawer, Find parts and Pending catalog all keep everything they have today.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first (1440px wide)
- Add, never replace: each new section sits inside a screen the owner already uses
- Every match explains itself in one plain sentence, with the numbers that made it
- A person always wins: anything entered by hand, kept or removed by the owner beats an automatic match, and the screen says so
- Nothing is blocked: unsure matches show "Needs checking", and the owner decides
- Every value has a source: each number, link and index shows a "Source" link
- Position and side always read the same way everywhere: two chips, Front/Rear and Left/Right/Both
- Plain words first; codes (OE, WVA, FMSI) appear only next to their values

INTERACTIONS:
- **Saving a number:** after saving a factory number, cross-reference or shared component, the row shows a small "Matching…" spinner chip, which turns into the new match count when done.
- **Side needs position:** Side stays disabled until a Position is picked; Side "Both" means left and right.
- **Matches link:** "Matches →" links open Find parts filtered to that car and part type.
- **Keep:** asks once (the drawer is the confirmation), then the row turns "Kept" and moves into the history.
- **Remove:** the row turns "Removed", and the match never comes back on its own.
- **Grouping rule:** removing a number or link removes only its automatic matches. Entries you made yourself are never changed.
- **Eye icons:** they open the existing vehicle inspector; part names open the existing Full database drawer.
- **Hover and focus:** rows highlight in the primary container colour; focus rings are 2px primary; Esc closes drawers and dialogs.
