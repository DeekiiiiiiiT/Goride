ROAM PLUG FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Plug (Public App):

Design a complete high-fidelity mobile app UI for "Roam Plug" — a free motoring companion app for drivers in Jamaica. Drivers use it to find gas stations and live fuel prices island-wide, track maintenance for their own vehicle by logging their odometer when they fuel up, find parts that fit their car, and find auto parts stores, mechanic shops, air pumps, restrooms and car washes near them. Drivers also help map Jamaica: they can pin any motoring place they are standing at, and every pin is reviewed by the Roam team before it appears on the map. The app is 100% free — nothing is sold in this version.

BRAND IDENTITY:
- Theme: "Sunset Orange" — energetic, enthusiastic and bold
- LIGHT MODE (default):
  - Primary: Vivid Orange (#ea580c) — main buttons, active tabs, Log Fuel button, links
  - Secondary: Warm Orange (#f97316) — secondary buttons, selected chips, map price bubbles
  - Tertiary: Golden Yellow (#eab308) — highlights, badges, contributor levels, the "Cheapest" price tag
  - Success / verified: #16a34a
  - Error / overdue: #dc2626
  - Warning / due soon: #d97706
  - Neutral: #57534e (muted text, icons, borders at low opacity)
  - Background: Warm off-white (#FAFAF9)
  - Surface cards: Pure white (#FFFFFF)
  - Text primary: Warm charcoal (#1C1917)
  - Gradient 1: linear-gradient(135deg, #ea580c, #eab308) — splash, hero headers, success screens
  - Gradient 2: linear-gradient(135deg, #f97316, #fbbf24) — promo/community cards, contributor badges
- DARK MODE:
  - Primary: #fb923c
  - Secondary: #fdba74
  - Tertiary: #fde047
  - Success: #22c55e
  - Error: #ef4444
  - Warning: #f59e0b
  - Neutral: #d6d3d1
  - Background: Warm dark gray (#292524)
  - Surface: #44403c
  - Gradient 1: linear-gradient(135deg, #9a3412, #a16207)
  - Gradient 2: linear-gradient(135deg, #c2410c, #ca8a04)
- Pending / awaiting approval: Neutral stone (#57534e) with a dashed outline (dashed pin on the map)
- Fuel grade chip colors:
  - E-10 87: Green (#16a34a)
  - E-10 90: Red (#dc2626)
  - Diesel / ULSD: Charcoal (#1C1917 on light, #d6d3d1 on dark)
- Use gradients sparingly (hero areas and celebration moments); keep lists and forms on flat white surfaces
- Clean, modern, minimalistic design language
- Practical motoring aesthetic — bold, warm, map-first, glanceable at the pump
- Typography: Inter or similar clean sans-serif; prices in tabular numerals
- Brand name: "Roam Plug"
- Logo treatment: "ROAM PLUG" wordmark with a small icon combining a location pin and a plug prong
- Currency always J$ (Jamaican dollars), distance in km, volume in litres

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Mobile-first (iOS/Android)
- Touch-friendly tap targets (minimum 48px — used one-handed at the pump)
- Safe area insets for notch devices
- Map-first discovery with a draggable bottom sheet list
- Bottom sheet patterns for place details and filters
- Large center "Log Fuel" action in the bottom navigation
- Status-driven UI for places: Verified (green check) | Unconfirmed (gray) | Awaiting approval (stone gray, dashed outline — only visible to the person who pinned it)
- Every price shows its age ("updated 2 h ago")
- Light theme default (high readability outdoors in sunlight)
- Optional dark mode variant
- Works offline: logs and pins are saved and sent later
- Jamaica context everywhere: parishes, towns, Plus Codes, +1 876 phone numbers, local brands (Total, Rubis, Texaco, Fesco, Cool Oasis, Petcom), local cars (Toyota Roomy, Honda Fit, Toyota Axio, Probox, Voxy, Hiace, Nissan AD Wagon, Suzuki Swift)
