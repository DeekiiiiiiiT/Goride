Roam Dominion - Parts database, Import by area (Desktop Admin, for the owner and catalog editors):

Design high-fidelity desktop screens that replace the Import tab of the Parts database in the Roam Dominion admin console. Today the owner drops any CSV into one box, it goes straight into the live catalogue, and nothing is checked. The new Import works one area at a time (Brakes, Engine, Filters and the rest). The owner picks an area, downloads that area's template, uploads the file, and sees every row checked before anything goes live: ready, ready with a label, errors with the line number, duplicates and unchanged. They fix or leave out bad rows, press "Import N rows", and every import is kept in an Import ledger per area with an exact Undo. Dominion is the master reference database: it never shows fleet names or plates. Parts are information only: no prices, suppliers, stock or quantities anywhere. The tone is a calm, careful control room: nothing goes live by surprise.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first (1440px wide)
- One area at a time: the area decides the template, the allowed part types and the checks
- Nothing goes live until the owner presses "Import N rows"; uploading only checks
- Bad rows never block good rows: errors are left out, good rows can still be imported
- Every problem says the line, the column and what to do, in plain words
- Every import is in the ledger, and Undo puts back exactly what was there before; when Undo isn't possible it says why
- A 4-step stepper at the top of the Import tab: "1 Area · 2 Upload · 3 Check · 4 Done"; done steps get a tick, the current step is filled primary

INTERACTIONS:
- Clicking an area card opens Screen 2 for that area; "← All areas" goes back without losing anything
- Choosing a file checks the header in the browser at once; "Upload and check" is enabled only when the header matches
- After upload the page moves to Screen 3 and shows the checking progress until the check finishes
- Clicking a summary chip filters the table to that status
- Unticking a row marks it "Left out" and lowers the number on "Import N rows"
- "Fix" turns a cell into an input; "Check row" re-checks only that row and updates the chips
- "Download failed rows" saves a CSV with the original columns plus error_code and error_message
- "Import N rows" asks nothing more, shows a short progress bar on the button, then opens Screen 4
- "Undo" in the ledger opens the drawer on its footer; "Undo import" in the confirm dialog runs it and the row changes to "Undone"
- Hover: rows highlight in the primary container colour; focus rings 2px primary
