Roam Dominion - Research Engine (Desktop Admin, for the owner, who is not a mechanic):

Design high-fidelity desktop screens for the research engine in the Roam Dominion admin console. Dominion is Roam's master database of cars, their sizes and fluids, and their parts, and every Roam app reads from it. Today the owner fills it by hand, one car at a time. The research engine now does that work. For a car version it looks up the car's exact identity, its specs (battery, oil, wipers, tyres, fluids) and its parts on trusted catalogue websites. It saves a copy of the page each answer came from and adds the answer to Dominion. The owner only steps in for three things: (1) a "Looks right" review of what Roam found; (2) a "browser check", where a catalogue website won't let Roam read it, so the owner opens the page, finds one line and pastes it; and (3) a "two makers disagree" decision. The engine fills the cars that matter first, so most cars will show "Not started yet" or "Waiting its turn" for a while. Those calm waiting states matter as much as the busy ones. The design must feel modern, seamless and effortless, like a premium consumer app, not a tool for engineers. Each screen is one view, drawn tall enough to show everything with no inner scrolling.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first at 1440px; content column max 1120px. One phone screen (390px) is included.
- Plain words first: "Front brake pads for the Toyota Roomy 2016–2020 2WD", never codes first.
- Every answer shows where it came from: the site's logo or name, "Read on 20 Nov 2026", and a "See the saved page text" button that opens the exact quoted line.
- One clear next action per row. Review actions sit at the bottom of the drawer in one row.
- Waiting is normal, not an error: waiting states are calm slate cards, never red.
- The owner's effort is shown and limited: a counter "Browser checks waiting for you: 7 of 25" appears wherever checks are listed.
- Undo is always offered after an add, inside a green toast and in the item's history.
- Right-side drawers are 560px wide and open over the list; the list stays visible behind a soft scrim.

INTERACTIONS:
- **Row chevron (Screens 1, 6):** opens that item's drawer; Esc or the close button closes it.
- **Looks right — add it:** adds the part, shows the green toast with Undo, and the row turns "Added".
- **Edit and add:** opens the same fields in edit mode inside the drawer, then saves.
- **See the saved page text:** opens the saved-text overlay above the drawer.
- **Open the Amayama page:** opens the outside site in a new browser tab; the drawer stays open.
- **Paste box:** as soon as text is pasted, Roam shows what it read under the box. Confirm turns blue only when the line contains the car and a number.
- **Use this one / Neither is right:** an in-app dialog confirms, then the drawer closes and the row updates.
- **Approve all new:** an in-app dialog "Add 2 new Roomy versions?" then a toast.
- **Fill this car / Fill every version / Research this model / Find this car's identity:** a toast "Roam queued 14 jobs for this car" and the tiles change to "Waiting its turn".
- **Toggles (Screen 9):** turning the engine off asks first in an in-app dialog.
- **Mark as readable / browser check, Remove, Turn off:** always an in-app dialog.
- **Filters and search:** the list updates as you type; filters can be combined with search.
- **Keyboard:** "/" focuses search; Esc closes the top drawer or dialog.
