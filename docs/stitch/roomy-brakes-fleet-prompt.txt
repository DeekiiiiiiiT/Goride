ROAM FLEET — FIND PARTS · BRAKES, ONE CARD PER PART, "ALSO FITS" — DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Find Parts, Brakes (Desktop Web App, for fleet managers and their staff):

Design high-fidelity desktop screens for the "Find parts" tab inside Fleet maintenance in the Roam Fleet app. A fleet manager picks one of their cars, opens Brakes, and sees every brake part their car has: front pads, rear shoes, rotors, drums, calipers, wheel cylinders, hoses, master cylinder, handbrake cable, ABS sensors and brake fluid. One physical part is shown as ONE card, with all the brand numbers that are the same part listed inside it. Opening a part shows every other car that same part fits — for example the Roomy's front pads also fit the Mazda 2, Suzuki Swift, Daihatsu Materia and Ford Fiesta — so the manager knows the part is common and easy to find.

Find parts is an information tool, not a shop: no prices, suppliers, stock, quotes or maintenance schedules. Everything already on Find parts stays as it is (the car picker "Which car is it?", the part picker, "Your parts activity", "Ask Roam to find it"). These screens change the Brakes list, the result cards and the part detail only. The tone is calm, plain and helpful: a parts encyclopedia for your own cars, written for someone who knows nothing about cars.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first (1440px wide), same page width and cards as the current Find parts tab
- One physical part = one card. Brand numbers that are the same part live inside the card, never as separate cards
- Every brake part type the car has is listed, even when Roam has nothing yet — never hide a type
- "Also fits" is grouped by maker so a long list stays easy to read
- Show trust plainly with the two chips. Never hide a Needs checking row
- Back / next arrows at the top of the page, never buttons at the bottom

ADDITIONAL UI COMPONENTS:

INTERACTIONS:
- **Brake type row:** clicking opens Screen 2 (parts on file) or Screen 4 (nothing on file).
- **Copy:** clicking a copy button copies that number and shows a small "Copied" toast.
- **"Also fits" filter:** All / Confirmed / Needs checking narrows the list; counts update.
- **"Needs checking" chip:** tooltip "Roam is confirming this. You can still use the number."
- **"Open source":** opens the maker's catalogue page in a new tab.
- **"Ask Roam to find it":** sends the request, the card switches to "Request sent".
- **Focus:** focus rings follow the existing Fleet style; every row and button reachable by keyboard.
