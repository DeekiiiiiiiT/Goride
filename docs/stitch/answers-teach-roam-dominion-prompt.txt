Roam Dominion - Your answers teach Roam (desktop admin console for the owner, who is not a mechanic):

Design high-fidelity, modern screens for the Roam Dominion admin console. The owner uploads a page saved from a car maker's parts catalogue, and Roam saves every part it can prove from the page. When the page uses a word Roam doesn't know yet (Toyota calls a strut mount "SUPPORT SUB-ASSY, FRONT SUSPENSION"), or doesn't say front or rear, Roam now asks one short question on a card that shows exactly what it read: the heading, the row, the number and the dates. One tap saves the part and Roam remembers the answer, so the same wording on the next page saves by itself, and the owner can see it happening: "Saved 1 row using your earlier answers." A list on the Part types page shows everything Roam has learned, with Undo on each. It must feel effortless and trustworthy, like a premium consumer app: one question per card, plain words first, the catalogue's own words shown exactly as written. Make each screen tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling and no cut-off boxes. Draw only the screens asked for in this message. Dominion is the master reference database: no fleet names, plates, drivers, vehicle counts, "used by", prices, suppliers, stock or quantities anywhere.
