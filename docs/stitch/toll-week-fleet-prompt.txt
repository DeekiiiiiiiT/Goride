ROAM FLEET — TOLLS IN WEEK RECONCILIATION — FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Tolls in Week Reconciliation (Desktop Web App with a phone layout, for the fleet owner, fleet manager and fleet accountant):

Design the toll part of "Week Reconciliation" inside Roam Fleet. Once a week, the fleet manager takes one Monday-to-Sunday week and answers one question: "Who pays each toll?" Roam now reads the toll files itself (the tag usage statement, the tag top-ups and Uber's weekly reports), lines up every toll with the Uber trip it happened on, and SUGGESTS the match. The person confirms the suggestions, picks the right trip when two trips fit, decides who pays for tolls with no trip, checks the photos of cash toll receipts that drivers send from the driver app, and then seals the week's tolls. Nothing is decided behind the person's back: Roam suggests, the person decides, and every decision can be undone until the week is sealed. A sealed week is frozen. Anything that arrives late (a toll the provider sent late, a corrected amount, or Uber taking a toll payment back) waits beside the sealed week and is moved into the week that's open now with one button. The person using this is not an accountant. Every row says what happened in plain words, the amount in J$, and the one or two buttons that settle it. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best business banking apps.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Roam suggests, the person decides. A suggestion is never applied on its own; every one has a "Confirm" button.
- One screen for the whole week. Uploads, checks, decisions and the seal all happen inside Week Reconciliation. No step sends the person to Data Center or another page, and there is no second stepper inside the Tolls step.
- Every row answers three things: what happened (time, plaza, car, driver, amount), what Roam thinks, and the button(s) that settle it.
- The money always adds up, and the page shows it: "Spend = Paid back by Uber + Driver pays + Fleet pays + Still to decide". The tiles, the progress line, the section totals, the seal box and the right column always show the same numbers.
- Everything can be undone until the week is sealed. After sealing, the week is frozen; late items wait beside it and move into the open week with one button, never by editing the sealed week.
- Problems are rows, not error messages: each says what's wrong in plain words and offers the fix.
- Nothing is hidden: empty sections stay visible with a green check instead of disappearing.
- Calm, not alarming: red only for things that block sealing, rejected receipts and danger buttons.
- One view per screen.

ADDITIONAL UI COMPONENTS:
- Toll row: tag or banknote icon, date and time, plaza, plate chip, driver name, amount right-aligned, method chip, then Roam's suggestion and the buttons. The row highlights on hover; the row itself is not a link.
- Trip pill: "Uber trip 2:09 PM–2:55 PM · card", grey outline, with the paid-back amount in green.
- Confidence chip: "Sure match" (green), "Likely match" (blue), "Two trips fit" (amber).
- Decision chip on decided rows: "Matched", "Driver pays", "Fleet pays", "Kept with no toll", "Receipt rejected".
- Receipt thumbnail 48px with a camera badge; it opens the receipt dialog.
- Sum line used in the seal box: each part labelled, in tabular figures, ending in a green check when it adds up.
- Toast at the bottom right after each action: "Matched" with a red-outline "Undo" button inside the toast.
- Empty-section line: green check + "Nothing to …", same height as one row.


INTERACTIONS:
- "Confirm" on a suggested match moves the row into "Decided" with a short slide, updates the tiles, the progress bar, the section totals, the seal box and the right column at once, and shows the toast with Undo.
- "Confirm all sure matches" (shown at the top of "Suggested matches" whenever sure matches exist) opens an in-app dialog listing them, with a primary "Confirm 5".
- Picking a trip that also fits another toll shows the amber note in the dialog; after confirming, the other toll's suggestion updates on its own.
- "Driver pays" and "Fleet pays" apply at once and show the toast; there is no extra confirm.
- "Check receipt" opens the receipt dialog; "Receipt is fine" moves the toll into the right section; "Reject receipt" opens the reason dialog.
- "Seal tolls" opens an in-app dialog: "Seal tolls for the week of 24 Aug 2026? After sealing, this week's tolls can't change. Anything that arrives late moves into the open week." with a primary "Seal tolls".
- If someone else changed a toll first, the action is refused politely (the "Someone else changed this toll" state), the list refreshes, and nothing is lost.
- On the phone, sections open one at a time and the seal bar stays visible.
