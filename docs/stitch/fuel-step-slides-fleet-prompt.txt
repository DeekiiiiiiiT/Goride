ROAM FLEET — FUEL STEP, ONE CARD AT A TIME — FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Fuel step in Week Reconciliation (Desktop Web App with a phone layout, for the fleet owner and fleet manager):

Redesign ONE area of step 5 "Fuel" inside "Week Reconciliation" in Roam Fleet. Today the Fuel step shows five long sections stacked on top of each other (1 Fuel checks, 2 Disputes and adjustments, 3 Fuel policy, 4 Distance and who pays, 5 Before you finalize, then the Fuel seal bar). The person has to scroll down and open each section one by one, which is tiring and easy to get lost in. The new design shows ONE section at a time as a large card. A short progress row at the top shows the five parts and where the person is. When they finish a part they click "Next" and the card slides to the next part; "Back" slides to the previous one. The last card, "Before you finalize", ends with the "Finalize fuel" button. Everything outside this area stays exactly as it is today: the app frame, the left step rail, the page title "Fuel" with "Is every fuel fill matched and priced?", the "Refresh Data" and "Refresh fuel" buttons, and the right-hand "Money Check" column. Only the area between the "Refresh fuel" row and the "Previous: Tag account" footer button changes. The person using this is not an accountant: each card says in plain words what it is for, what needs doing, and the one or two buttons that do it. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best business banking apps. Every screen is tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- One part at a time. Only one card is on screen; the others are reached with Next / Back or by clicking a part in the progress row.
- The person always knows where they are and what is left: the progress row shows all five parts with their status ("Done", "1 to fix", "Review", "Nothing to do").
- Moving is never blocked: Next always works, so the person can look ahead. Only "Finalize fuel" waits until every part is clear, and the last card says exactly what is still open with a button that jumps to that part.
- Every card answers three things at the top: what this part is for (one sentence), its status chip, and what to do now.
- Problems are rows with their fix button next to them, never error messages.
- The money always adds up and the right-hand Money Check stays visible the whole time.
- Calm, not alarming: red only for things that block Finalize.
- Slides are short and smooth (250 ms, ease-out, the new card slides in from the right on Next and from the left on Back).
- One view per screen.

ADDITIONAL UI COMPONENTS:
- Progress row: five fully rounded pills on one line, joined by a 2px line; each pill shows number, short name and a status (green check "Done", red dot "1 to fix", amber "Review", slate "Nothing to do" / "Check", grey padlock "Not ready"). The current pill is near-black with white text. Every pill is a button that slides to that part.
- Thin step bar under the pills (4px, rounded, near-black fill).
- Part card: white, 20px radius, soft shadow, 28px padding; header = title + subtitle on the left, status chip on the right; content; footer row with Back, "Part N of 5", Next.
- Next button text always names the next part: "Next: Fuel policy →".
- Fix row: icon, plain sentence, amount on the right, one primary fix button.
- Empty state: green check circle, one sentence, one optional secondary button.
- Fuel seal bar: padlock, title, status chip, short reason, primary "Finalize fuel".
- Toast at the bottom right after an action inside a card: "Marked reviewed" with an "Undo" button inside the toast where the action can be undone.


INTERACTIONS:
- "Next" slides the current card out to the left and the next card in from the right (250 ms); "Back" does the reverse. The progress row and bar update at the same moment.
- Clicking a pill in the progress row slides straight to that part.
- The step opens on the first part that still needs something; if everything is done it opens on "Finalize".
- Fixing something inside a card (Resolve, Mark reviewed, Accept this gap) updates that card's chip, the matching pill and the Money Check at once, without leaving the card.
- On the Finalize card, "Go to …" buttons slide to that part.
- "Finalize fuel" opens an in-app dialog: "Lock fuel for the week of 5 Oct 2026? Fuel bought J$28,350.00 · Driver pays J$3,930.38." with a primary "Finalize fuel"; then Screen 7.
- The keyboard works too: left and right arrow keys move Back / Next when no text box is focused.
- On the phone the Back / Next bar stays at the bottom of the screen.
