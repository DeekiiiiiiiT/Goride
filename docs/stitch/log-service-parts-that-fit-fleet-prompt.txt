ROAM FLEET — LOG SERVICE · PARTS THAT FIT THIS CAR — FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Log service · Parts that fit this car (Desktop Web App with a phone layout, for fleet owners, fleet managers and their staff):

Design a new "Parts that fit this car" panel inside the existing "Log service" dialog of Fleet maintenance. The person using it is a normal fleet owner, not a mechanic: they never know a part number and never type one. When they pick a job that is a part (for example "Oxygen sensor") and say where on the car it is ("Front (before converter)"), Roam shows them the parts that fit their car, as simple cards: the maker's logo, the part's plain name, one number to show at the parts store, and a badge saying whether it is the part made for their car or another part that also fits. They tap one card to choose it. If Roam doesn't have that part for their car yet, the panel says so in plain words and offers one button, "Ask Roam to find it"; after the tap it says "Roam is looking for it. We'll tell you when it's added." and Roam later sends a notification "Roam added …". Because people usually buy the part first and fit it later, the panel also offers "Plan it": the job goes on the car's "Not done yet" list with the chosen part, and "Log it now" later opens the job with that part already chosen, so they only add the price and the labour. The old boxes on part jobs ("Brand", "Part number", "Link part (optional)") are gone. Nothing about parts is for sale: no prices, no shop links. The dialog keeps its four steps: 1 Date & mileage, 2 Work done, 3 Details, 4 Review. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best consumer banking and shopping apps: generous spacing, soft 12–16px rounded cards, soft shadows, larger friendly type, clear hierarchy, very few numbers on screen. Draw each screen tall enough that every element is visible, with no inner scrolling and nothing cut off.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Roam does the knowing. The person only picks the job and where it is; Roam shows what fits. Nothing on any screen asks for a part number.
- One clear next step per state: choose a part, or ask Roam, or plan it. Never two primary buttons.
- Plain words a non-mechanic understands: "Made for your car", "Also fits", "Check before buying", "Roam is looking for it", "Plan it", "Log it now".
- The panel sits right under "Where on the car", because the parts depend on it. Until a place is picked, the panel says "Pick where on the car to see the parts that fit."
- Information only: no prices, no stock, no shop names, no "buy" buttons.
- Saving is never blocked by parts: a job can always be saved with no part chosen ("Skip — I don't know the part").
- Everything else in the dialog stays exactly as it is today.

INTERACTIONS:
- The panel appears only on part jobs (jobs that are a part, like Oxygen sensor, Brake pads, Battery). Service-only jobs (oil change labour, inspection) look exactly as today.
- Picking or changing "Where on the car" reloads the cards for that place. If the chosen card no longer fits the new place, it is un-chosen.
- Tapping a card chooses it; tapping the chosen card again un-chooses it. Only one card can be chosen per job.
- The chosen part is saved with the job; the person never types its name or number.
- "Ask Roam to find it" sends the car, the job and the place to Roam's team in one tap, then shows "Roam is looking for it". If someone else already asked for the same part on the same car model, the person sees the same "Roam is looking" state. Asking never blocks saving.
- When Roam adds the part, everyone who asked gets the "Roam added …" notification; tapping it opens this car's parts for that job.
- "Plan it" opens the overlay; "Add to Not done yet" closes the dialog's job (J$ 0, nothing charged) and shows a green toast "Added to Not done yet for Toyota Roomy". "Log it now" later opens the job with the part already chosen.
- "Skip — I don't know the part" collapses the panel to one muted line "No part chosen" with a secondary "Show parts" button.
- On the phone, cards stack full width, chips wrap, and the bottom bar is sticky.
