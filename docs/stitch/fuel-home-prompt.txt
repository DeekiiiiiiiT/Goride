------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERALL LOOK — high fidelity, modern

A modern operations overview in the Dominion design system: calm, airy and confident, like Linear or Stripe's dashboard home. Use these surfaces and styles:
- Page background: a very light cool grey.
- Cards: white, with a hairline 1px border, a 12–16px radius and a very soft shadow.
- Spacing: a strict 8px grid with generous whitespace.
- Type: the Dominion sans for text, and the Dominion mono with tabular figures for every number, price, date and count.
- Big KPI numbers: large and light-weight, not heavy blocks.

Colour is restrained:
- Neutral greys almost everywhere.
- Dominion blue for links, primary buttons and progress.
- Green only for "Current".
- Amber for warnings and "Due".
- Muted red only for "Stale".
- Price differences use ▲ / ▼ with neutral text.

Micro-details: hairline dividers, small pill chips, a subtle hover lift on cards, focus rings and skeleton shimmer.

Keep it real: no glassmorphism, no gradients on data, no 3D, no illustrations, no AI badges, no "REGION" badges or "LOC:" footers.

Shell: the existing Dominion admin shell. The left sidebar is dark navy with "Fuel" selected under MASTER DATA. The top bar is white with the search field "Search entities, engines, routes…" and the owner avatar "DT · Deeki T · Owner".
