Project: Dominion Redesign (7083594865949473827) · Design system: Dominion
These screens sit beside the finished S36 "Jamaica map" Country screen. They MUST reuse its exact header, tab bar, KPI strip, card style, map style and inspector style, so the section reads as one product.

Make:
- S36c Level browser, in three versions: Parishes, Towns and Communities.
- S36d Border editor: Half Way Tree selected, a draft being compared to live.
- S36e Publish dialog.
- S36f Map pins.
- S36g Imports and checks: a run drawer open, and the stepper at step 2.
- Every screen in Light and Dark.
- A 390-wide phone version of S36c (Communities) and of S36f.
- One frame of states.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SHARED (copy from S36 exactly)

Look:
- The modern, calm GIS console of S36.
- Cards: white, a hairline border, 12–16px radius and a very soft shadow, on a very light cool-grey page.
- Spacing: a strict 8px grid.
- Type: Dominion sans for text. Dominion mono with tabular figures for every code, count, area and date.

Colour:
- Neutral greys, plus Dominion blue for selection and primary actions.
- Green only for "Live" / "All matched".
- Amber only for drafts and warnings.
- Muted red only for "broken" and danger.

Map line styles, the same as S36:
- Parish: dark slate, 2px.
- Town: mid-grey, 1.25px.
- Community: light grey dashed.
- Selected area: 2.5px blue outline with a 10% blue fill.

Shell: the Dominion admin shell, with the dark navy sidebar and "Places and Maps" selected.

Header (every screen):
- Breadcrumb "Dominion › Master data › Places and Maps › Jamaica map".
- Title "Jamaica map", with the subtitle "The official map of Jamaica, from the country down to communities."
- Right side: the green chip "Published" and the mono text "Updated 24 Oct 2026".

Tab bar (every screen):
- "Country · Parishes · Towns · Communities", then a divider, then "Pins · Imports".
- The current tab is selected with the solid Dominion-blue pill.
- Far right: the mono text "EPSG:4326 · WGS84".

KPI strip (every screen): "Parishes 14 · Towns 131 · Communities 775 · Drafts 0 · Quality issues 0 · Last change 24 Oct".

Keep it real:
- No glassmorphism, gradients, 3D or AI badges.
- No "REGION", "PARTITION" or "LOC" labels.
- No delivery or market words, except inside the Publish dialog's "Rush" count row.
