Roam Dominion - Vehicles home (Desktop Admin, for the owner and catalog editors, who are not mechanics):

Redesign only the first page of the "Vehicles" section in the Roam Dominion admin console: the page you land on before you pick a maker or a car. Dominion is Roam's master database. Vehicles holds every car version in it (1,833 versions across 56 makers), and the Roam apps read their cars from here. This page shows the database itself and nothing from the apps: no fleets, drivers, customers, requests or usage. Today the home page is a search box, a makers card, two "Recently opened" cards and a "Needs attention" card. It looks empty and tells the owner nothing about the state of the catalogue. The new home is a calm dashboard. At a glance the owner sees how big the catalogue is, how complete it is (which cars have their key parts and specs on file), what changed lately, and what needs a person. It must stay uncluttered: a few clear cards, plenty of white space, every number a button that opens the list behind it. KEEP the "Needs attention" card and the "Recently opened" cards; they move into the new layout but keep their content and behaviour.
