PART 1 — Roam Dominion - Price Engine (Desktop Admin, for the owner):

Design high-fidelity desktop admin screens for the "Price Engine" page of Roam Dominion — the master database admin for Jamaica. The Price Engine turns fuel prices reported by Roam Fleet drivers (from a photo of the pump) and Roam Plug users (from their fuel logs) into one trusted price per station and fuel grade. Nothing is ever deleted: prices that look unusual go to a Review queue where the owner approves or rejects them in one click. The owner must be able to see at a glance which prices are live, how confident each one is, and why any report is waiting for review.

BRAND IDENTITY:
- Match the existing Roam Dominion admin style exactly:
  - Left sidebar in dark navy (#1E293B) with white text; active item is a filled blue pill (#2563EB)
  - Sidebar sections: MASTER DATA (Vehicles, Places and Maps, Fuel (active), Tolls, Tax and Vendors) | ENGINES (Fuel Brain, Toll Brain, Matching Brain) | DISTRIBUTION | PLATFORM | BACK OFFICE
  - Top bar: search "Search entities, engines, routes..." with ⌘K hint, dark-mode toggle, avatar "DT — Deeki T, Owner"
  - Page sits in one large white card on a light gray background (#F1F5F9)
  - Breadcrumb: Dominion › Master data › Fuel › Price Engine
  - Small "REGION: JM-KGN" pill top right of the card
  - Numbers, prices and codes in a monospace font (like "JMD $201.8128"); everything else Inter
- Primary action / links: Blue (#2563EB)
- Approve: Green (#16A34A)
- Reject: Red (#DC2626)
- Needs review / warning: Amber (#D97706)
- Neutral / waiting: Slate (#64748B)
- Source tags: "Fleet" = navy chip (#1E293B, white text) with a truck icon; "Plug" = orange chip (#EA580C, white text) with a phone icon
- Confidence pills: High = green, Medium = amber, Low = slate; "May be outdated" = slate with a clock icon
- Fuel grade chips: E-10 87 green (#16A34A), E-10 90 red (#DC2626), Diesel charcoal (#1C1917), ULSD charcoal outline
- Currency always JMD / J$, volume in litres, Jamaica parishes and places (Half Way Tree, Spanish Town Road, Portmore, Constant Spring, Moneague)
- Station names in the real format used in Dominion: "TotalEnergies, Half Way Tree", "Texaco, Molynes Road", "FESCO Spanish Town Road", "RUBiS Half Way Tree", "Petcom | Moneague", "Jampet, Willowdene"
- Light theme default, dark mode variant

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first (1440px wide), keyboard friendly
- Tabs across the top of the page: Overview | Review (with count badge) | Published prices | Reporters | Settings
- Every number explains itself on hover (tooltip with how it was worked out)
- Petrojam wholesale price for the grade is always shown next to a reported price, so the owner can judge it instantly
- Nothing is hidden: rejected reports stay visible in a "Rejected" filter
- Keyboard shortcuts shown on buttons (A approve, R reject, G change grade, N next)

PART 2 — Roam Plug - Fuel price states (Mobile app updates):

Design high-fidelity mobile screens for Roam Plug, a free motoring companion app for drivers in Jamaica. These screens update how fuel prices are shown and logged. Prices on the map are no longer one driver's number: Roam Plug combines reports from many drivers and the Roam Fleet into one trusted price per station and grade, shows how confident it is, and says plainly when a price may be outdated. When a driver logs fuel, their price is checked first and appears on the map shortly after.

BRAND IDENTITY:
- Theme: "Sunset Orange" — energetic, enthusiastic and bold
- LIGHT MODE (default):
  - Primary: Vivid Orange (#ea580c)
  - Secondary: Warm Orange (#f97316)
  - Tertiary: Golden Yellow (#eab308) — the "Cheapest" tag
  - Success / verified / High confidence: #16a34a
  - Error: #dc2626
  - Warning / Medium confidence: #d97706
  - Neutral / Low confidence / May be outdated: #57534e
  - Background: #FAFAF9, Surface cards: #FFFFFF, Text: #1C1917
  - Gradient 1: linear-gradient(135deg, #ea580c, #eab308)
  - Gradient 2: linear-gradient(135deg, #f97316, #fbbf24)
- DARK MODE: Primary #fb923c, Secondary #fdba74, Tertiary #fde047, Success #22c55e, Error #ef4444, Warning #f59e0b, Neutral #d6d3d1, Background #292524, Surface #44403c
- Fuel grade chips: E-10 87 green (#16a34a), E-10 90 red (#dc2626), Diesel / ULSD charcoal (#1C1917 light, #d6d3d1 dark)
- Typography: Inter; prices in tabular numerals
- Brand name "Roam Plug"; currency J$, km, litres
- Jamaica context: stations like "Texaco Barbican", "Total Constant Spring", "Rubis Hope Road", "Cool Oasis Portmore"; cars like 2019 Toyota Roomy, Honda Fit, Toyota Axio

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Mobile-first, 48px minimum tap targets, safe areas
- Every price shows: the price, its age, and its confidence — glanceable at the pump
- Confidence shown with simple words, never jargon: "Confirmed by 3 drivers" (green), "Confirmed by 2 drivers" or "From a fleet driver" (amber), "1 driver report" (gray)
- A price that may be outdated is still shown, greyed slightly, with a clock icon and "May be outdated"
- The driver never sees another person's raw report — only the combined price
- Calm, honest wording: the app never says "wrong", it says "checking"
