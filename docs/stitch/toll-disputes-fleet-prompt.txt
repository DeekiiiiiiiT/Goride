ROAM FLEET — TOLL DISPUTES IN DRIVER SETTLEMENTS AND FLEET POLICY — FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Disputes tab in Driver Settlements, and the Tolls tab in Fleet Policy Configuration (Desktop Web App with a phone layout, for the fleet owner and fleet manager):

Design two new areas of Roam Fleet for toll disputes. The rule behind them: on a trip, the passenger always owes the toll, never the fleet. Uber pays the toll back to the driver; when Uber pays short (the Portmore toll costs J$390.00 and Uber paid back J$370.00), the driver asks Uber for the remaining J$20.00 from the Uber driver app within 9 days of the trip. Roam opens these disputes by itself and the driver sees each one in the Roam Driver app. Area one is a new "Disputes" tab in Driver Settlements, next to "Cash desk", "Close Week" and "Restatements": the owner sees every open dispute of every driver, which ones are not sent yet, which are waiting on Uber, which are late, and can mark one as sent for a driver, close one as lost, or jump to the week the toll belongs to. Refunds are not linked here (that happens in Week Reconciliation). Area two is a new "Tolls" tab in Fleet Policy Configuration, next to "Rules" and "Schedule": the owner sets who pays when a dispute is lost or runs out of time, each platform's toll rules (does it pay tolls back, does it take disputes, how many days to dispute), and each toll plaza's price per platform with the date it starts. The person using this is not an accountant: every card says in plain words what it is for. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best business banking apps. Every screen is tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Each screen answers "what needs me now?" first: counts and amounts at the top, then the list, oldest and most urgent first.
- The days left are always visible on a dispute; the chip turns red with 2 days or fewer left.
- "Late" is a warning, not a loss: it means "not sent with 2 days or fewer left" or "sent, one pay period passed and no refund yet". A refund can still come, so "Lost" is never the main button.
- Disputes are about money still owed by Uber; every row shows "Toll · Uber paid · amount to dispute" in that order.
- Settings read like plain questions with plain answers ("When a dispute is lost, who pays?" — "The fleet" / "The driver"), never field names.
- Prices change over time: a new price never overwrites the old one; it is added with the date it starts, and the old prices stay listed underneath.
- Nothing saves without the "Save" button; leaving with unsaved changes asks first, in-app.
- Calm, not alarming: red only for late disputes and missing settings that stop disputes from opening.
- One view per screen.

INTERACTIONS:
- Clicking a driver chip filters the list; "All drivers" clears it. The tiles always count every driver.
- "Mark as sent" opens Screen 3; confirming moves the row to "Sent · waiting on Uber" at once and updates the tiles and the tab badge.
- "Lost" opens Screen 4; if the change needs a second person, Screen 5 appears next, then the row leaves the list.
- "Open week" opens Week Reconciliation on the week the toll belongs to, at the Tolls step, part 3 "Disputes".
- On the Tolls tab, any change shows "1 change" in the footer and enables "Save"; "Save" opens Screen 11; leaving with unsaved changes asks "Leave without saving?" in an in-app dialog.
- "Add rate" opens Screen 8; a new rate is added with its start date and never replaces the old ones.
- A row in the price table opens Screen 9.
- A "Missing" line's "Add rate" opens Screen 8 filled in for that plaza and platform.
- The keyboard works throughout: Tab moves between buttons, Enter confirms dialogs, Escape closes them.
