Roam Dominion - Maintenance templates, 3 tabs remade (Desktop Admin, for the owner and catalog staff):

Redraw three tabs of the Maintenance templates section of the Roam Dominion admin console: "Service packages", "Review list" and "History". The "Part schedules" and "Jobs" tabs are already designed — these three must look like the same product (same cards, chips, buttons and spacing as the Jobs tab you designed: job rows with icon, name, mono code, small grey chips, outline buttons). In this section a JOB is one piece of maintenance work (e.g. "Spark Plugs", "Wheel Alignment"); a PART SCHEDULE says how often a job is due (e.g. every 40,000 km or 24 months); a SERVICE PACKAGE is one garage visit that only lists which jobs are done together and never sets how often a job is due; jobs are arranged into SECTIONS inside a group (e.g. Engine → Air Intake & Induction); "ALSO DO" links suggest jobs that go together. Dominion never shows customer names, fleet names, plates, car counts, prices, suppliers or stock. Draw each screen at FULL HEIGHT so every element is visible; nothing may be cut off, blurred or hidden.
