Roam Dominion - Maintenance templates: owner's manuals and part schedules (desktop admin console for the owner, who is not a mechanic):

Design high-fidelity, modern screens for the Maintenance templates area of the Roam Dominion admin console. Dominion decides how often every part of every car is serviced, and every app in Roam uses those numbers. From now on the car maker's owner's manual is the main source. The owner picks a car model, uploads its owner's manual as a PDF once, and says which pages hold the maintenance table. Roam reads those pages in the background, then shows each page next to the numbers it found: every part with its normal schedule and its harder-driving schedule (taxis and heavy traffic). The owner checks them and approves, and every car of that model uses them from then on. A model with no manual yet uses Roam's own rule, labelled "Roam policy". Each number always says where it came from, down to the page: "Owner's manual · page 414". It must feel effortless and premium, like a modern document app: big clear page previews, calm progress, one obvious next step. Make each screen tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling and no cut-off boxes. Draw only the screens asked for in this message. Dominion is the master reference database: no fleet names, plates, drivers, vehicle counts, "used by", prices, suppliers or stock anywhere.
