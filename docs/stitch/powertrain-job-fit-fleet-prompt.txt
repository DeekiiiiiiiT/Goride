ROAM FLEET — LOG SERVICE: JOBS THAT FIT THIS CAR FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Log service · Jobs that fit this car (Desktop Web App, for fleet owners and their staff):

Design modern, premium screens for step 2 "Work done" of the "Log service" dialog in Fleet maintenance in the Roam Fleet app, now that it only offers the jobs that fit the car being serviced. The person using it is a normal car-fleet owner, not a mechanic. A petrol Toyota Roomy no longer sees high-voltage battery jobs or diesel-only jobs. A Honda Fit hybrid sees its hybrid jobs but not plug-in charging jobs. Nothing is ever blocked: a "Show all jobs" button at the bottom reveals the hidden jobs, greyed with a small "Not usual for this car" chip, and they can still be ticked with no warning pop-up. If the app doesn't know what powers a car yet, it shows every job with a friendly banner and a way to fix it. A job logged even though it isn't usual for the car keeps its "Not usual for this car" chip on the Details step, on Review and in Service history. The dialog keeps its four steps: Date & mileage, Work done, Details, Review. The app should feel like a polished consumer product in the style of Linear, Stripe and Airbnb: friendly, airy and easy to scan. Each screen is one single view, light mode only (dark mode comes from the Stitch theme switch). Make every screen tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling and nothing cut off.

BRAND IDENTITY:
- Roam Fleet web app: a clean top bar with "Roam Fleet"; the page "Fleet maintenance" with a friendly orange wrench icon; the Log service dialog floats over a softly blurred, dimmed page
- LIGHT MODE (default):
  - Primary: Near-black (#030213) — primary buttons, the current step, selected jobs
  - Background: very light grey (#f8fafc); cards and dialogs white
  - Text: #0a0a0a; helper text soft grey #71717a
  - Success: Emerald (#059669)
  - Attention: Amber (#d97706)
  - Danger (Cancel): Red (#d4183d)
  - Soft tints for pills and icon tiles: Slate 100 (#f1f5f9)
- DARK MODE (for reference; draw light only):
  - Background: #18181b; cards: #232327; text: #fafafa; helper text: #a1a1aa; primary: #fafafa; success #34d399; attention #f59e0b; danger #f87171
- Shapes: cards and dialogs 16px rounded corners; buttons and inputs 10px; chips are fully rounded pills
- Depth: soft, light shadows on cards and dialogs; at most a very light 1px border
- Typography: Inter. Titles 20–24px semibold, body 15px, helper text 13px. Sentence case everywhere. Numbers use lined-up (tabular) figures.
- Icons: friendly line icons (Lucide style) on soft-tinted round tiles
- Chips: "Not usual for this car" soft amber with a small info icon; the car's powertrain on the car strip as a soft pill ("Petrol", "Hybrid", "Electric") with a matching icon (engine, battery-charging, bolt)

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Modern and friendly: generous white space, clear hierarchy, nothing cramped
- Plain words for a normal person: "Jobs that fit this car", "Show all jobs", "Not usual for this car". No codes or technical labels
- Never block: hidden jobs are one tap away and can always be logged; no warning pop-ups
- Every action is a real button with a fill or a visible outline, never a text link. One solid black primary button per view. Cancel is red (red outline, red text)
- No browser pop-ups; every message is inside the app
- Desktop-first (1440px), with a phone version

INTERACTIONS:
- Step 2 opens with only the jobs that fit the car; groups with no fitting job are left out and the grid closes up
- "Show all jobs (N hidden)" reveals the hidden jobs under the "Not usual for this car" divider; "Hide jobs that don't fit" hides them again (ticked hidden jobs stay ticked and visible)
- Ticking a hidden job works like any other: no pop-up, no confirmation
- A ticked job that isn't usual keeps its chip on Details, Review and in Service history
- On a car with an unknown engine type, every job is shown with the banner; "Link this car" opens the existing link-to-model flow
- If the fit check fails, every job is shown with the "Couldn't check" strip and a "Try again" button
- Esc closes the dialog; focus returns to the button that opened it; every tile, card and button works by keyboard
