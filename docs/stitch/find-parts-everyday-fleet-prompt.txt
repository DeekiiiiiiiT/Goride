ROAM FLEET — FIND PARTS FOR EVERYDAY PEOPLE — FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Find Parts (Desktop Web App with phone layouts, for car owners, fleet owners and their staff):

Design a complete, modern, high-fidelity redesign of the "Find parts" tab inside Roam Fleet. The person using it knows only three things about a car: the make, the model and the year. They are not a mechanic and have never seen a parts catalogue. They pick their car, say what they need (for example "front brake pads"), and get ONE plain answer: the part, a Verified badge, and one number to give the parts store. They can also see which other cars use the same part, shown by maker logos, so they know the part is common and easy to buy. Find parts is a look-up tool only: Roam does not sell parts, so there are no prices, shops, stock, quotes or carts anywhere. The feeling should be as easy and pleasant as a modern consumer app: big friendly type, maker logos, simple part pictures, lots of white space, one clear next step on every screen, and zero codes or jargon.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first at 1440px inside the Fleet page, plus phone layouts at 390px. Content column max 1120px, centred, with 32px gutters.
- One question per screen, asked in plain words, with one obvious next step.
- Picture first, words second: maker logos for cars, line illustrations for parts.
- One part = one card. All brand numbers for the same part live inside that card, folded away.
- One number to give the store, always the most trusted one, shown big with a Copy button.
- Show where the part sits on the car with a tiny top-down car drawing that highlights the wheel or area (front left, rear right, both rear wheels). This replaces every FRONT/REAR tag.
- Every part type the car has is listed, even when we have nothing yet ("Not on file yet — ask us").
- Trust is a badge, never a sentence of reasons. Internal reasons never appear in Fleet.
- Back arrow and the car chip sit at the top of every screen; there are no buttons at the bottom of the page.
- Calm motion: cards fade and lift slightly on hover, sections slide in, Copy shows a small "Copied" toast.
- Fully readable in dark mode; tap targets at least 48px on phone.

INTERACTIONS:
- **Car card / maker logo / model / year:** each tap moves to the next question with a short slide.
- **Version cards (2WD / 4WD / I'm not sure):** picks the version; "I'm not sure" keeps both and marks differences.
- **Area tile / part row / search result:** opens the part answer (Screen 6/7) or "We don't have this one yet" (Screen 10).
- **Copy number / Copy icon:** copies and shows the "Copied" toast for 2 seconds.
- **"Other numbers stores may use":** opens and closes in place.
- **See every car:** opens the right panel; maker tiles expand in place; filter pills narrow the list and update counts.
- **Badges:** tap or hover opens the small in-app popover; "Got it" closes it.
- **Ask Roam to find it / Ask Roam to add this car:** sends the request; the card turns into "Request sent".
- **Open the maker's page ↗:** opens the outside website in a new tab.
- **Back arrow:** always one step back; the car chip opens "Which car is it for?".
- **Keyboard:** every tile, row and button reachable with Tab, Enter opens, Esc closes panels and popovers, visible focus rings.
