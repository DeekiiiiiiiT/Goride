Roam Dominion - Find Parts (Desktop Admin, for the owner and data staff):

Design high-fidelity desktop admin screens for "Find parts", the first tab of the Parts database in Roam Dominion. A person picks a car (make, model, year), optionally picks the part they need, and sees every part that fits, grouped into five match types. The flow is written for someone who knows nothing about cars: plain words, big choices, one question at a time, and a question is only asked when the answer changes the result. In Dominion, staff use the same search to see exactly what a customer would see, and every gap becomes an "Add it now" action. No prices and no customer data appear in Dominion.

- Match the existing Roam Dominion admin style:
  - dark navy left sidebar
  - white top bar with the search field "Search entities, engines, routes…" and the owner avatar "DT · Deeki T · Owner"
  - white cards and soft rounded inputs on a cool off-white page

  - Primary: Dominion Blue (#0153dc) — primary buttons, links, the selected choice
  - Primary container: Pale Blue (#dbe1ff) — selected cards and chips
  - Success / verified: Green (#006e31)
  - Warning / needs checking: Amber (#d97706)
  - Error: Muted Red (#a83836)
  - Neutral: Slate (#797a84) — borders, muted text, icons
  - Background: Cool off-white (#faf8fe)
  - Surface cards: Pure white (#FFFFFF)
  - Text primary: Charcoal (#30323b)
  - Sidebar: Dark navy (#0f172a) with white text; the active item ("Vehicles") is a solid Dominion Blue pill
- DARK MODE:
  - Primary: #5b8def
  - Primary container: #1e3a5f
  - Success: #4ade80
  - Warning: #f59e0b
  - Error: #f87171
  - Neutral: #94a3b8
  - Background: Deep navy (#0b1220)
  - Surface: #111a2e
  - Text primary: #e2e8f0
- Five match-type accent bars (thin 4px left edge on each result section):
  - 1 Made for your car: #0153dc
  - 2 Same car, different badge: #0e7490
  - 3 Fits your engine: #b45309
  - 4 Other brands that replace it: #7c3aed
  - 5 Fits by size: #006e31
- Status chips:
  - "Verified": green dot
  - "Needs checking": amber outline
  - Small grey tag for the technical match name ("OEM", "Sister", "Engine", "Aftermarket", "Universal")
- No gradients, glassmorphism or 3D. No AI badges, no confidence percentages, no "REGION" badges or "LOC:" footers. No prices anywhere.
- Typography: Inter for text. Part numbers, chassis codes, counts and years use the mono font with tabular numerals.
- Jamaica context: Japanese imports (Toyota Roomy, Honda Fit, Toyota Probox, Nissan Note); brands like Toyota, MANN-FILTER, NGK, Denso, NWB

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first (1440px wide), with a phone version
- One question per step, with big tappable cards, not dense forms
- Ask "Which one is yours?" and "Front or back?" only when the answer changes the result
- Plain words first and the trade word second: "Made for your car" with a small "OEM" tag
- The part is optional: the person can always skip to "Show everything for this car"
- A sticky trail of choices ("Honda Fit 2018 › 1.5 hybrid › Brake pads › Front") lets the person jump back to any step
- Show only match types that have results; fold the empty ones into one line
- An empty answer is never a dead end: it always offers "Add it now"
- Light theme default, dark mode variant
- Strict 8px spacing grid, 12–16px card corners, hairline borders, very soft shadows, skeleton loading

- Sticky choice trail with edit chips
- Search input with a suggestions dropdown ("also called …")
- Part tile and area tile (icon, label, count)
- Problem row that expands into likely-part cards
- Car outline diagram with front/back highlight
- "Best match" card
- Result section with a coloured left bar, plain title, count pill, technical tag and explanation
- Folded "Nothing on file for …" strip
- Status chips: Verified, Needs checking
- "Fits all 3 / Only the 1.5 hybrid" version chip
- Right-side detail drawer with a sticky footer
- Calm empty state with a primary "Add it now"
- Ranked list editor with drag handles
- Skeleton loaders, error card, info strip
- Toasts: "Part confirmed", "Saved — kept in History"

INTERACTIONS:
- Choosing make, model and year moves straight to step 3 when every version gives the same answer, and to step 2 only when the answers differ
- "Not sure" shows merged results with a version chip on every row
- Typing in the search waits until the person pauses, then shows suggestions; picking one skips to results (or to the side question)
- Clicking an area tile expands its parts inline; clicking a problem expands its hint and likely parts
- "Front or back?" appears only for parts that come in front and back versions
- "Skip — show everything for this car" jumps to Screen 8
- Every choice chip in the trail reopens its step and keeps the later choices if they still apply
- The URL keeps the car, version, part and side, so a search can be shared and reloaded
- Clicking a result row opens the detail drawer; Esc closes it
- "Add it now" opens the add-part form already filled with the car, version, part and side
- Owner-only buttons are disabled for staff, with a tooltip explaining why
