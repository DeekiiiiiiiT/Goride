Roam Dominion - Pending Catalog (Desktop Admin, for the owner):

Design high-fidelity desktop screens for "Pending catalog" in the Roam Dominion admin console. Today it lists only requests for new cars. It now becomes one queue with two tabs, Vehicles and Parts. The Parts tab collects the requests fleet managers send from Roam Fleet's Find parts tab when a part isn't on file for their car ("Ask Roam to add this part"). The owner sees which car versions need which parts, how many fleets asked, and any notes or photos. They look up the right part number, add it to the parts database straight from the request, and every fleet that asked sees it the next time they search. Dominion is the master reference database, so these screens never show customer names, company names or plates: requests are grouped by car version and part, with a count of how many fleets asked. The tone is a calm, efficient work queue.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first (1440px wide)
- One queue, two tabs, newest first
- Group duplicate asks: one row per car version + part + side, with "Asked by {n} fleets"
- Every request can be finished from its drawer in one or two clicks
- Never show who asked; show how many asked

SCREEN 1: PENDING CATALOG — PARTS TAB
- Dominion frame as in Brand Identity
- Page title "Pending catalog", sub line "Cars and parts that apps have asked us to add."
- Tabs: "Vehicles (1)" and "Parts (4)", with Parts selected
- Summary cards on top: "New 3", "Looking 1", "Added this week 0", "Can't find 0"
- Filter chips: "All", "New", "Looking", "Added", "Can't find"; a search box "Search car or part…"
- Table, newest first, with columns:
  - Car version ("Toyota Roomy 2016–2020 · Pre-Facelift · 1KR-FE · 2WD")
  - Part ("Air filter", "Headlight bulb", "Brake fluid", "Rear brake shoe kit · Left")

  - Status chip
  - Row action "Open"
- The Vehicles tab (not selected) keeps today's list of new-car requests

SCREEN 2: PART REQUEST DETAIL (DRAWER OVER SCREEN 1)
- Right drawer, 520px
- Header: "Air filter" and "Toyota Roomy 2016–2020 · Pre-Facelift · 1KR-FE · 2WD"; status chip "New"; "Asked by 3 fleets · first asked 31 Oct 2026"
- Section "What they asked":
  - each request's note, numbered and without names: "Note 1: 'old one says 17801-…'", "Note 2: —"
