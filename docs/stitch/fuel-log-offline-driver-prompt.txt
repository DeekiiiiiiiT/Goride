==================== FIRST PASTE ====================

Roam Driver - Log fuel with or without signal (Phone App, 390px, for drivers of fleet cars):

Design high-fidelity phone screens for the new "Log fuel" flow in the Roam Driver app. A driver at the pump takes a photo of the odometer, picks how they paid, takes a photo of the pump or receipt, and taps Save. That's it. The fill is saved on the phone straight away, even with no signal, and sends by itself when the signal comes back. Roam reads the numbers from the photos, so typing is optional. Each fill in the list shows where it is: "On this phone", "Sent", "Read" or "Needs a look". If the app closes in the middle, the driver picks up where they left off with their photos still there. The tone is calm, short and reassuring: the driver should never wonder whether the fill was saved. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best consumer banking apps. Every screen is tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling. Draw only the screens asked for in this message.

  - "Sent" blue with an up-arrow icon

- Buttons always look like buttons:
  - Primary = solid emerald with white text, full width, 52px tall.
  - Secondary = white with a visible #cbd5e1 border and dark text.

  - Never underlined text links, never grey text-only buttons. Confirms are in-app bottom sheets, never browser pop-ups.

  - Optional number box: label above ("Kilometres", "Total (J$)", "Litres"), a 52px tall input, and a muted "Optional" tag on the right of the label.
  - Payment choice card: icon on the left, title, one helper line, a radio circle on the right; the chosen one has an emerald border and a soft emerald wash.
  - Fill card in the list: line 1 date and time with the plate box; line 2 the amount big (or "Amount coming" in muted text when not read yet) and the litres beside it; line 3 the status chip and the payment ("Cash", "Gas Card", "Gas Card + Cash"); a chevron on the right.
  - Toast: dark, rounded, bottom of the screen, a white tick, 2 seconds.
- No gradients, glassmorphism or 3D. No AI badges, no sparkles.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Three steps, one question each: odometer photo → how did you pay → pump photo → Save.
- The photo is the main thing on every photo step. Typing is always optional and sits below the photo.
- "Saved" always appears right away. Sending happens on its own and is shown only as a status chip, never as a spinner the driver has to wait on.
- No loading screens anywhere in this flow. No "locking location", no "reading the photo" waits.
- Never mention databases, servers, AI or "Roam is looking". Say what happened in plain words: "Saved on this phone", "Sent", "Your fleet will check it".
- Large tap targets for one-handed use at the pump.
- One view per screen.

INTERACTIONS:
- Spec: docs/fuel-log-offline-first-audit.md (FO-01..FO-17, section 5 Screens).
- The Fuel tile on Select Category starts the location in the background (20 s limit, FO-03). Location never shows on screen except the "Location — Saved / Not saved" row on a fill.
- Take a photo = native camera, width 1600 (FO-02). The photo is written to the outbox at once (FO-01). Preview uses an object URL, never a data URL.
- Number boxes are filled by the background reading only if it answers within 15 s (FO-04); the driver can type over it. A typed value always wins.
- Next on Odometer is enabled once a photo exists. Save is enabled once the pump photo exists (Cash, Gas Card + Cash) or straight away (Gas Card).
- Save writes the fill to the outbox, shows the "Saved on this phone" toast and opens the Fuel list. The outbox sends it (FO-05..FO-10). The chip moves On this phone → Sent → Read / Needs a look.
- Send now runs the outbox at once. There is no remove or clear anywhere (FO-18); an item leaves the phone only when the server confirms it arrived.
- Pick up where you left off appears when the outbox holds an unfinished fill (after a screen kill or app close, FO-01 / FO-17).
- Where did you fuel? appears after Save only when a GPS fix arrived and no station is within range; Skip closes it.
- Needs a look: Add numbers saves the typed total/litres onto the same fill (same captureId). Retake pump photo replaces the pump photo on the same fill.
- The signal pill shows when the last send attempt failed or the phone reports no connection.
- Same screens are reused in the Roam Fleet driver section (apps/fleet/src/components/driver-portal) through the shared package (FO-16).
- Removed for good: "Locking your station location…", "Skip for now", "retry GPS", "Continue without GPS — pick station", "Reading the photo…", "Sync Now", "Force Retry All".
