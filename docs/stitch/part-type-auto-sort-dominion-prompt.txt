Roam Dominion - Roam sorts unknown parts by itself (desktop admin console for the owner, who is not a mechanic):

Design high-fidelity, modern screens for the Roam Dominion admin console. The owner uploads a page saved from a car maker's parts catalogue, and Roam saves every part on it. Until now, every part Roam had no type for became a card the owner had to judge ("Add part type" or "Not a part we track") — 62 of them on one heater page, which nobody but a mechanic could answer. Now Roam decides by itself, once per part name, and remembers the answer for every car after: parts a workshop replaces on their own (a blower motor, an expansion valve) become new part types and are saved with their pictures; ducts, brackets, covers and fixings are simply not tracked. The owner is never asked. The result shows one calm summary line, and a review list on the Part types page lets the owner flip any answer with one button. It must feel effortless and trustworthy, like a premium consumer app: no cards to judge, one quiet line, a clear list. Make each screen tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling and no cut-off boxes. Draw only the screens asked for in this message. Dominion is the master reference database: no fleet names, plates, drivers, vehicle counts, "used by", prices, suppliers, stock or quantities anywhere.

INTERACTIONS:
- Review → Part types page, Sorted by Roam card, filtered to that saved page.
- Don't track → POST part-names/:key/skip (plan R4); row moves to Not tracked; if Roam made the type and nothing else uses it, the type is hidden from Fleet lists (G9); its saved parts stay.
- Track it → POST part-names/:key/track; saves on at most 10 uploads per press and answers `remaining`; button then reads "Track on {remaining} more uploads".
