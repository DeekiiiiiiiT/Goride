Design one desktop screen for "Station analytics" inside the existing Dominion admin shell (dark navy left sidebar, white top bar with search, "Fuel" selected in the sidebar). Use the Dominion design system: neutral greys for almost everything, one blue accent for pump prices, a muted slate for the Petrojam benchmark, amber and red only for things that need fixing. Numbers use the mono font already used on Dominion KPI cards. Cards have 12px corners, a 1px light border and a soft shadow. The look is calm and dense, like a professional market-data terminal, not a marketing dashboard.

PURPOSE: a data owner checks what fuel costs at Jamaican pumps this week, how far above the Petrojam refinery price it is, how much of the island has a price, and whether the data can be trusted. There is no customer, fleet, driver or vehicle information anywhere on this screen.

- Filter bar (one row): Grade dropdown "E10-87", Week dropdown "Petrojam week · 22 Oct 2026", Parish dropdown "All parishes", Brand dropdown "All brands", and a text link "Reset".
