Roam Fleet - Add a Vehicle (Desktop Web App with a phone layout, for the fleet owner and fleet manager):

Design the new "Add a vehicle" flow inside Roam Fleet, plus the places where a vehicle's picture and documents show afterwards.

How the flow works:
- The fleet owner scans two Jamaican documents: the Certificate of Fitness, then the Motor Vehicle Registration.
- Roam reads them, shows what it read so the person can fix anything, then asks them to confirm which car it is (maker logo, model, generation, years).
- After saving, the vehicle gets a beautiful studio picture of that exact car in that exact colour. If Roam already has that picture it appears at once; if not, Roam makes it in a few seconds.
- Roam reads the documents and keeps the details, not the files. Every scan screen says so in one calm line.

Who uses it:
- Fleet owners in Jamaica, adding Japanese-import cars such as the Toyota Roomy and Honda Fit.
- They are not mechanics and not tech people. Every screen asks one thing, in plain words, with one obvious next button.

Look and feel:
- Modern, effortless and premium, like the best consumer car apps: generous spacing, large friendly type, soft shadows, rounded cards, and the car picture as the hero.
- Each screen is tall enough to show everything with no inner scrolling.
- Draw each screen in light mode. Dark mode uses the colours below.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first at 1440px; the add card is 760px wide and centred. Two phone screens (390px) are included.
- One question per screen. The main button always says exactly what happens next ("Looks right — next", "This is my car").
- Roam fills in everything it can read. The person only checks and corrects.
- Every field Roam read gets a tiny blue "Read by Roam" tick on the right; a field the person changed shows "Edited".
- A field Roam could not trust (for example a date that doesn't make sense) is empty with an amber "Check this date" hint under it, never a wrong value.
- The car picture is the reward at the end and the hero of the vehicle card; it never blocks saving.
- Plain, warm wording. No tech terms. Errors say what to do next.
- Generous white space; nothing cramped; at most two buttons side by side, with Cancel on its own at the left.

ADDITIONAL UI COMPONENTS:
- Step bar: four steps with numbers, labels and connecting lines; done = green check, current = solid near-black, later = muted.
- Drop zone: dashed border, illustration, two buttons inside, a highlighted state when a file is dragged over (border turns near-black, wash turns #f1f5f9).
- "Read by Roam" tick: tiny blue check + the words, 12px, right side of an input. "Edited" in slate replaces it after a change.
- Colour chip: rounded, a 12px filled dot in the colour (white and pearl white get a thin grey ring), then the colour name.
- Colour dropdown: the 16 colours as rows with their dot and name, the current one checked.
- Car picture frame: white, 16px radius, 4:3, with either the studio picture or the grey outline + label; a shimmer only while "Making your picture".
- Number plate field: letter-spaced bold text in a rounded box with a thin border.
- Notice cards: soft amber or soft blue, 16px radius, icon on the left, one bold line and one plain line.
- Status chips: "Active" (green), "Inactive" (slate), "Valid" (green), "Expires soon" (amber), "Expired" (red), "Picture ready" (green).
- Privacy line: lock icon + one muted sentence.


INTERACTIONS:
- Choosing or dropping a file goes straight to "Reading your certificate…" (Screen 2), then to the check screen.
- The colour chip opens the 16-colour dropdown; picking one replaces the chip and shows "Edited".
- "Looks right — next" moves one step forward; "Back" moves one step back and keeps everything typed.
- "Pick a different car" opens an in-app dialog with three dropdowns (Make with logos, Model, Year), then shows matching car cards to choose from.
- "This is my car" saves the vehicle and opens Screen 8 if the picture already exists, otherwise Screen 7.
- On Screen 7 the picture frame swaps to the studio picture by itself when it's ready (a soft fade, no page reload). After 60 seconds without it, the label changes to "Still making it" and a "Check again" button (secondary) appears.
- "Type the details" opens the check screen with empty fields instead of read ones.
- "Change colour" on the vehicle page opens the same 16-colour dropdown in a small in-app dialog with "Save" (primary) and "Cancel" (red outline); after saving, the picture frame shows "Picture coming" until the new picture arrives.
- "Update" on a document opens the add flow at that document's scan step and returns to the Documents tab when done.
- With "reduce motion" turned on, the shimmer and the fade are off.
- Cancel on any step asks in an in-app dialog: "Stop adding this vehicle? Nothing will be saved." with "Keep going" (secondary) and "Stop" (solid red).
