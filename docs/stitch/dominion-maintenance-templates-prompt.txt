ROAM DOMINION — MAINTENANCE TEMPLATES (SERVICE SCHEDULES) FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Dominion - Maintenance templates (Desktop Admin, for the owner and catalog staff):

Design high-fidelity desktop screens for the Maintenance templates section of the Roam Dominion admin console. Dominion is the master reference database. Here the owner decides how often each part of a car needs service: oil every 5,000 km or 3 months, brake fluid every 40,000 km or 24 months, and so on. The Roam Fleet app reads these rules to build every customer car's due list and alerts. Today the section has 5 service packages (Inspection, Basic, Intermediate, Major, Long-Term) and the bug is that a part takes the interval of the shortest package it sits in, so brake fluid inside "Basic Service" was treated as a 5,000 km job. The new design separates two ideas clearly: a PART SCHEDULE (one part, how often, for which cars, and where that number came from) and a SERVICE PACKAGE (which parts are done together at one garage visit — it never sets how often). A part schedule can be for everyone (all cars), for one engine, for one platform (chassis code, e.g. M900A), for a whole platform family (e.g. the M900 family: Toyota Roomy, Daihatsu Thor, Subaru Justy), or for one exact car. The most specific one wins. Every schedule says where its number came from — the car maker's manual, the part maker, or "Roam policy" (the owner's own rule) — and every change is kept in history. Before any change that would move due dates on real customer cars, the screen says how many Roam Fleet cars will change and asks to confirm. Dominion never shows customer names, fleet names or plates: only counts.

The feel: calm, exact, uncluttered — a well-kept service manual, not a spreadsheet. One question per screen, one primary action, plain words first with the trade term small and muted beside it (e.g. "Every 5,000 km or 3 months · whichever first", "Platform · chassis code").

INTERACTIONS:
- "+ Add schedule" opens Screen 4 empty; a row on Screen 1 opens Screen 3; "Add" on a ladder step opens Screen 4 with that "Which cars" choice preset
- Changing "Which cars" shows how many catalogue cars it covers under the field as you type
- "Make active", "Retire" and "Reactivate" open Screen 5 first when any Roam Fleet car would change; when none would, they save straight away with a toast
- "Save as draft" never changes any car; drafts show the "If you make this active" preview
- Saving a schedule for cars that already have an active one saves it as "Needs checking" and adds it to the Review list with a toast "Saved for review — pick one in Review list"
- "Keep A" / "Keep B" asks "Retire the other schedule? Cars using it move to the one you keep." then shows Screen 5 if cars change
- Package editor changes never open Screen 5 (they don't move due dates)
- Support users see "Send to owner for approval" instead of saving live changes; the owner sees it in Review list
- Hover: rows highlight in the primary container colour; focus rings 2px primary; every field has a visible label
- Esc closes drawers; unsaved drawer asks "Leave without saving?" with "Keep editing" / "Leave"
