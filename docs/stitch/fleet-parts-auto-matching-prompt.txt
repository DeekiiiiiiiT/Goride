ROAM FLEET — FIND PARTS · "WHY IT FITS" — DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Find Parts, "why it fits" (Desktop Web App, for fleet managers and their staff):

Design high-fidelity desktop screens that update the result rows of the "Find parts" tab inside Fleet maintenance in the Roam Fleet app. Roam now matches parts to cars automatically from factory part numbers, sister cars and parts shared between car families. Each result now says, in one plain sentence, why it fits the person's car, and shows when Roam is still checking a match.

Find parts stays an information tool, not a shop: no prices, suppliers, stock, quotes or maintenance schedules. Everything already on the Find parts screens stays exactly as it is (the car picker, the part picker, the side question, the five sections, "Your requests" and the "Ask Roam to add it" flow). Only the result row gains a "why" line and a status chip. The tone is calm, plain and helpful: a parts encyclopedia for your own cars.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first (1440px wide)
- Change only the result row; everything else on Find parts stays exactly as it is
- Every result says why it fits, in one short sentence a person who knows nothing about cars can read
- Show trust plainly: "Confirmed", or "Roam is checking". Never hide a result
- Numbers appear only to back up the sentence, never instead of it
