Roam Fleet - Fuel Review Queue and Transaction Logs (Desktop Web App, for the fleet owner and fleet manager):

Design the remaining fuel screens of Roam Fleet so they match the new "Needs a look" card the owner already likes. Two pages. The Review Queue is where the owner approves or rejects drivers' fuel fills: first the fills that need a look (already designed), then the fills ready to approve, then a window to check one fill and approve or reject it. Transaction Logs is the list of saved fills, with three views: Transactions (every fill this week), Full Tanks (fill-to-fill tank cycles), and Awaiting statement (gas card fills waiting on the gas card statement). The person using this is not an accountant or a mechanic: every card says in plain words what it is for and what to do. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best business banking apps, with generous spacing, soft shadows and large clear type. Every screen is tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling. Draw only the screens asked for in this message.
