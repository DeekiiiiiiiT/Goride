Roam Fleet - Fuel step in Week Reconciliation, the driver's fuel lines in Driver Settlements, and the Rules tab in Fleet Policy Configuration (Desktop Web App with a phone layout, for the fleet owner and fleet manager):

Design the new fuel screens for Roam Fleet. Every week, each car's fuel is split between the company and the driver by the fleet's fuel rule. Until now Roam priced each kilometre at the car's usual fuel economy, so in a good week the purposes could add up to more fuel than was bought. The new way is simple and exact: cost per km = the fuel bought ÷ the kilometres on the odometer. Each kind of driving (Uber trips, driving to pickups, commute, km nobody can explain) is its km × that cost per km. The company's part and the driver's part always add up to exactly the fuel bought, to the cent. The driver's charge is a short list of lines, one per kind of driving, each one showing km × cost per km × the rule's driver %. The screens also catch problems before the week locks: a gas-card fill still waiting for its card statement, a stretch so long a fill was probably not scanned, and a car whose economy suddenly changes. The step 5 "Fuel" screen already exists as five cards, one at a time (Fuel checks · Disputes · Fuel policy · Distance and who pays · Finalize). Keep that exactly, and only add what each screen below asks for. The person using this is not an accountant: every card says in plain words what it is for. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best business banking apps: generous spacing, soft elevations, rounded cards, clear hierarchy. Every screen is tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling. Draw only the screens asked for in this message.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- The money always adds up, and the screen shows it: every money card ends with the adds-up line.
- Every charge can be traced: the driver's amount is never a lone number, it always sits next to its km × cost per km × driver %.
- Problems are rows with their fix button next to them, never error messages. Each one names the car and the date.
- Waiting is calm (amber), not alarming. Red only for things that block Finalize, or for money that doesn't add up.
- A locked week is read-only and shows the numbers it was locked with.
- One view per screen.
