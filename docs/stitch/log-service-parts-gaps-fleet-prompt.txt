ROAM FLEET — LOG SERVICE · WHERE ON THE CAR + PARTS ROAM DOESN'T HAVE YET — FULL DESIGN PROMPT (GOOGLE STITCH)




Roam Fleet - Log service · Where on the car, and parts Roam doesn't have yet (Desktop Web App with a phone layout, for fleet owners, fleet managers and their staff):

Design the changes to the existing "Log service" dialog of Fleet maintenance that let a fleet manager record exactly what was done, even when Roam doesn't know the part yet. Two things change. First, some jobs happen in one of several places on the car: an oxygen sensor is either in front of the exhaust converter or behind it; wiper blades are on the driver side, the passenger side or the back; a headlight bulb is low beam or high beam. For those jobs the job card now shows a row of choice chips under the job name ("Front (before converter)", "Rear (after converter)", "Not sure"), and the person must tap one before saving the job. "Not sure" is always there, for when the garage's paper doesn't say. Wheel jobs (Tire Change, Brakes, Brake rotor) keep today's four wheel buttons exactly as they are. Second, when a part isn't in Roam's list, the person can still log it with "+ Other item" or "Type it in instead", and can now add the part number from the box or the receipt. After the service is saved, Roam sends that part to its own team to look up and add to the parts list, and the service history shows that it was sent. The same position chips also appear on the "What the inspection found" rows, so a garage's "front oxygen sensor" can be recorded as not done yet and carried into "Log it now". The dialog keeps its four steps: 1 Date & mileage, 2 Work done, 3 Details, 4 Review. The person using this is a normal fleet owner, not a mechanic: plain words, no codes. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best business banking apps: generous spacing, soft 12–16px rounded cards, soft shadows, clear hierarchy. Draw each screen tall enough that every element is visible, with no inner scrolling and nothing cut off.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Ask only what matters: position chips appear only on jobs that can be in more than one place; every other job looks exactly as it does today.
- Never stuck: "Not sure" is always a choice, so the person can always save.
- Say what will happen before it happens: a part Roam doesn't have shows "Roam will look this part up…" before saving, and the history shows what really happened after.
- Plain words for places on the car: "Front (before converter)", "Driver", "Low beam" — never technical codes.
- Add to the existing screens, never take anything away: every part of today's Log service dialog, Items used, Pick a part and service history stays as it is.
- Calm, not alarming: amber for waiting, red only for errors and Remove.
- Desktop-first (1440px), with a phone layout. 8px spacing grid, soft shadows, skeleton loading.
- One view per screen.

INTERACTIONS:
- The chips show only on jobs that can be in more than one place; on any other job nothing changes.
- Tapping a chip picks it; tapping it again unpicks it. Several places can be picked on any job (both oxygen sensors, both wipers). Picking "Not sure" unpicks all others; picking a place unpicks "Not sure".
- "Save line →" stays disabled until at least one chip is picked; the reason line disappears as soon as one is.
- Typing a name in Other item, or using "Type it in instead", shows the indigo line "Roam will look this part up…" under it at once. Removing the item removes the line.
- Nothing is sent while the dialog is open; the part goes to Roam only after "Save service". The service history then shows the status line, and it updates by itself from "Sending to Roam…" to "Sent…" within a couple of minutes.
- "Log it now" from a found job opens Log service with the same place already picked (Screen 6). If that place no longer applies to the job, nothing is picked and the chips ask again.
- On the phone, chips wrap, fields stack, and the bottom bar is sticky.
