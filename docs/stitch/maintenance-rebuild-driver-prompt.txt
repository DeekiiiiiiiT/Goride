Roam Driver - My car kit: service due, do not drive, and the pre-shift check (Phone App, 390px, for drivers of fleet cars):

Design high-fidelity phone screens for the "My car kit" part of the Roam Driver app. Today the driver sees the car's inventory and can start a pre-shift check, but cannot see what service is coming up, and is not told when the fleet has stopped the car. Now the top of My car kit shows a "Service due" card with the next three things the car needs, in plain words ("Oil change · in about 870 km"), and a "Report a problem" button that sends a request straight to the fleet. When the car must not be driven, a clear red banner says so, with the reason and a button to call the fleet. The pre-shift check fills in the odometer from the last reading, shows the checklist for this kind of car, and after a failed safety item says plainly that the car is now stopped. The tone is friendly, short and reassuring, never a telling-off. The design must feel modern, calm and effortless, like the best consumer apps. Every screen is tall enough to show everything, with no inner scrolling. Draw only the screens asked for in this message.

- One clear next step per screen. Large tap targets for one-handed use.
- One view per screen.

Empty state = "Nothing due right now" + next item.
- DoNotDriveBanner: fleet.vehicles.do_not_drive + reason + set_at + who; blocks "Start pre-shift check" only when the car is stopped.
- ReportProblemSheet: issue chips + urgency + notes + photo → createMaintenanceRequest (existing, no callers today, MER-16). "Can't drive it" urgency also notifies the fleet at once.
- DVIR: template picked by the car's class (MER-17), odometer prefilled from the last reading (source dvir when changed), Fail on a Safety item requires a photo and shows the stop notice before sending.
- Notifications: getMaintenanceDriverNotifications (existing, no callers today).
- Dark mode: exact colour values.

INTERACTIONS:
- My car kit loads schedule + do-not-drive state; refresh on pull-down.
- "Report a problem" → S4 → "Send to the fleet" → S5; the request appears in Fleet → Maintenance → Requests.
- Pre-shift: "Send check" enabled when all items answered and the odometer is filled; Fail on a Safety item → after send, S8 (car stopped), and My car kit shows S2.
- "Call the fleet" opens the phone dialer with the fleet support number (tel:), not a browser pop-up.
- Fleet clearing do-not-drive sends the "Car cleared to drive" notification; banner disappears on next load.
- Drivers never see costs, garage bills or other drivers. Drivers can't remove a report (Cancel only before Send).
