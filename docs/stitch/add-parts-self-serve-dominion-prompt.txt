Roam Dominion - Parts database, "Add parts" (Desktop Admin, for the owner and catalog editors, who are not mechanics):

Design a complete, modern, high-fidelity redesign of the "Add parts" tab in the Roam Dominion admin console. The real job is always the same: the owner has a parts catalogue page open (for example Toyota's own catalogue on Partsouq) that shows a diagram and a list of part numbers for one car, and wants to get every part on that page into the database for that car and its sister cars. Today that takes one full 4-step trip per part, retyping the same link, the same car maker and the same cars every time, clicking trust car by car, and asking someone where the next question is. Adding the brakes for one car took a whole day. The new flow starts from the page: paste the link once, pick the cars once, then add each part on the page as a quick row, check one review, and save everything in one go. The app works out everything it can from the link (which car maker, genuine or not, how trusted). It asks only one plain question at a time, right where you are looking. A first-time owner must finish it alone, without anyone guiding them, to the standard of a team of UI/UX experts. Dominion is the master reference database: no fleet names or plates, and no prices, suppliers, stock or quantities anywhere.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES:
- Desktop-first at 1440px; content column max 960px, centred. A 390px phone frame is included for checking on the go.
- Start from the catalogue page, not from one part. Link and cars are entered once and reused for every part on that page.
- One question at a time, revealed in order directly under the last answer (never at the bottom of the page, never in a separate box elsewhere).
- The app answers what it can by itself and shows it as a filled, editable answer with a small "Worked out from your link" note.
- Several things at once: paste many chassis codes, tick many cars, set trust for all ticked cars, add many part rows.
- Compact lists: one line per car or part, expand to edit. Changing years never changes trust.
- The page is its own guide: a short hint under each question with a real example; "Next" is always clickable and, if something is missing, scrolls to it and says what's needed in red under that field.
- A slim stepper at the top: "1 The page · 2 Cars · 3 Parts · 4 Review". Done steps get a tick and can be clicked to go back.
- Nothing is saved until "Save all"; every save can be undone from "Recently added".
- The existing "Trusted sources" tab, "Full database" and "Find parts" tabs stay as they are.

INTERACTIONS:
- **Paste a link:** the app reads it at once and fills site, catalogue, genuine and diagram; each has a "Change" button.
- **Car search:** typing several codes separated by commas or spaces finds all of them; ticking a model's "Tick all" ticks every version under it.
- **Add sister cars:** ticks the matching versions of Tank, Thor and Justy and adds them to Selected.
- **Years "Change":** opens a small from–to editor in place; saving it changes only the years.
- **Part type picked:** the "Where on the car" buttons change to fit that type; the saved name preview updates.
- **Part number typed:** checked against the database as you type; already-on-file shows the blue info line.
- **Paste several numbers:** each pasted line becomes a row with the last-used part type.
- **Mark some as Needs checking:** shows checkboxes; the bulk bar applies one choice and one reason to all ticked cars.
- **Next:** always clickable; if something is missing, scrolls to it and explains in red.
- **Save all:** saves every part and car in one go; on failure nothing is saved and all rows stay.
- **Add more from this page / Same cars, new page:** start again with the link and/or cars kept.
- **Undo (Recently added):** in-app dialog "Undo this save? 3 parts and 6 fits will be removed." with red "Undo save".
- **Cancel:** in-app dialog when anything is unsaved.
- **Keyboard:** Enter adds a new part row from the last cell; Tab moves across a row; Esc closes menus.
