SECTION 02 FITMENT ALGORITHMS

Three ways a part fits

01
Specific cars

For pads, shocks, filters. You tick exactly which car models and chassis fit.

02
By engine

For oil filters, spark plugs, belts. Any car carrying that engine gets it automatically.

03
By size or spec

For bulbs, wipers, batteries, fluids, tires. Fits any vehicle whose technical spec matches.

SECTION 03 DATA INTEGRITY

Which sources count as Verified?

verified VERIFIED SOURCE

Car maker's official parts catalogue or the part maker's own catalogue (e.g. s-mk.com, toyota-epc.jp).

schedule NEEDS CHECKING

Distributor portals, retailer lookup, forum lists, or aftermarket “equal parts” cross-reference sheets.
