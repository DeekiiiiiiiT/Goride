Reconciliation Plain-English Guide

sync_alt Where the money came from vs. Who ends up paying

Every fuel entry starts at a physical fuel terminal. The pump is charged either through an integrated fleet card (FleetCor / Comdata) or directly paid out-of-pocket by the operator.

Rule of thumb: Total Fuel Bought = Company Expense + Driver Charges + Quarantined Variances.
payments Why cash from earnings is credited back to drivers

When drivers pay cash at the pump for commercial route legs, Roam Fleet verifies the odometer reading and GPS proximity. Validated cash expenses are reimbursed back directly on their weekly settlement slip.

security How unexplained fuel is quarantined until resolved

If a transaction exceeds tank capacities (e.g., 540L pumped into a 500L tank) or skips an odometer node, Roam Fleet quarantines that specific dollar difference ($150.50 currently). It cannot hit the general ledger until an admin approves or rejects the variance.
